G-7 Agrees to Release 100 Million Barrels of Diesel and Crude - WSJ

PARIS—The Group of Seven major economies agreed to release 100 million barrels of crude oil and fuel from their emergency stocks together with allied nations and not to restrict oil exports, the group said, in an effort to bring down the soaring price of diesel and other fuels.
On his Truth Social platform, President Trump said that European nations agreed “to release a massive amount of their heavily stocked Diesel Oil. The process will begin immediately.”

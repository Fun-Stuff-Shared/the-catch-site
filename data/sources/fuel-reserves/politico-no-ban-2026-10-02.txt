Trump rules out diesel export ban after Europe agrees to tap stockpiles - POLITICO

The president made the announcement hours after Europe caved to U.S. pressure and agreed to release from its stockpiles of the fuel.
10/02/2026 05:30 PM EDT
President Donald Trump said Friday he was ruling out a diesel export ban and never really considered one in the first place, hours after Europe caved to U.S. pressure and agreed to release fuel from its stockpiles.
The announcement appears to put to rest more than a week of frantic lobbying across two continents after surging fuel prices — caused by the ongoing war against Iran as well as Ukrainian attacks against Russian refineries — have angered farmers and put pressure on the Trump administration to act ahead of the November midterm elections.
“Europe has a lot of diesel, and they’re going to be making a major world contribution, and so are we,” Trump told reporters as he left the White House for a campaign trip. “And we’re not going to be doing the export ban.
“It was never really ever on the table, but what Europe did was a great thing,” he added.

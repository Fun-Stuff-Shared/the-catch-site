                                 A BILL


 
  To prohibit the export of diesel fuel from the United States during 
                certain periods, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Diesel Price Reduction Act of 
2026''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON EXPORT OF DIESEL FUEL DURING CERTAIN PERIODS.

    (a) Prohibition.--Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the 
Secretary of Commerce shall prohibit the export of diesel fuel to any 
country during any period described in subsection (b).
    (b) Period Described.--The period described in this subsection is 
the period that--
            (1) begins on the date that is the fourteenth consecutive 
        day on which the average retail price of diesel fuel in the 
        United States is greater than $5 per gallon; and
            (2) ends on the first day after the date described in 
        paragraph (1) that is the thirtieth consecutive day on which 
        such price is less than $4.50 per gallon.

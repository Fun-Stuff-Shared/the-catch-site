How reporting works.

  1. What raised your concern?

    A clinic is billing Medicaid for patient visits that never happened.

    Tell us what happened

    Describe what happened in a few sentences: what you saw, who was involved, and when the fraud occurred.

  2. Review your report

    What happened
    Who was involved
    Amount
    Supporting documents
    Contact information

    Add documentation and review

    We’ll compile a report. Check the details, fix anything that’s off, and attach up to 5 documents or photos.

  3. Your report has been submitted.

    Keep a copy of your report and reference number for your records.

    Reference number

    DEMO-2026-001

    Submit and download a copy

    Your report goes to the federal office best situated to act on it. You get a reference number and can download a copy.

What happens next.

  1. Treasury Department reviews it

    Your report is reviewed and sent to the federal office best situated to act on it.

  2. Investigators may reach out

    If you share contact information, the office may ask for more details.

  3. Action stops the fraud

    That can mean a criminal charged, a payment stopped, or a provider suspended.

  4. You may qualify for a reward

    If you include your contact info and your report leads to criminal charges or recovering money stolen by fraudsters, you may be eligible for an award.

We send your report to the right office.

Each federal office handles different kinds of fraud. We read what you wrote and match it to the agency that can act on it.

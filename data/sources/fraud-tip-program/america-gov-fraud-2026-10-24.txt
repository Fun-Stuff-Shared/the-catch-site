How reporting works.
- Tell us what happened- Describe what happened in a few sentences: what you saw, who was involved, and when the fraud occurred.
- Add documentation and review- We’ll compile a report. Check the details, fix anything that’s off, and attach up to 5 documents or photos.
- Submit and download a copy- Your report goes to the federal office best situated to act on it. You get a reference number and can download a copy.
What happens next.
- Treasury Department reviews it- Your report is reviewed and sent to the federal office best situated to act on it.
- Investigators may reach out- If you share contact information, the office may ask for more details.
- Action stops the fraud- That can mean a criminal charged, a payment stopped, or a provider suspended.
- You may qualify for a reward- If you include your contact info and your report leads to criminal charges or recovering money stolen by fraudsters, you may be eligible for an award.

Detention of Applicants for Admission | U.S. Customs and Border Protection

Effectively immediately, applicants for admission encountering the Department of Homeland Security for the first time after failing to lawfully present for inspection at a U.S. port of entry are subject to mandatory detention under INA Section 235. Commissioner Rodney S. Scott issued the guidance to all U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) components on July 10, 2025.

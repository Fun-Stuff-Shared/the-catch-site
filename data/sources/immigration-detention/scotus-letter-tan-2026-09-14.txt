Dear Mr. Harris,

       I write to notify the Court formally of a recent development relevant to the
certiorari-stage briefing in the three above-captioned cases. On September 11, 2026,
the Court dismissed Genalo v. G.M., No. 25-886, which was to be argued on October
13, 2026. The dismissal of Genalo relates to the three pending certiorari petitions in
that the Respondents in Putra and Rhoney, and the Petitioners in Buenrostro-
Mendez, have argued, in part, that the Court should not take up the constitutional
claim presented by the Petitioners in the former two cases because a related
constitutional issue was before the Court in Genalo. See Br. in Opp’n 3, 19, 24–25,
Putra v. Lopez-Campos, No. 25-1415 (Sup. Ct. Aug. 24, 2026); Resp. to Cert. Pet. 3,
17–19, Rhoney v. Barbosa da Cunha, No. 26-104 (Sup. Ct. Aug. 21, 2026); Cert. Pet.
4, 29–30, Buenrostro-Mendez v. Blanche, No. 26-43 (Sup. Ct. July 2, 2026).

       Notwithstanding the dismissal of Genalo, the Putra and Rhoney Respondents
and Buenrostro-Mendez Petitioners maintain that the Court should grant only on the
statutory issue presented in Buenrostro-Mendez and Rhoney for the reasons already
set forth in the briefs in these three cases. See Br. in Opp’n. 3–4, 18–23, Putra v.
Lopez-Campos, No. 25-1415 (Sup. Ct. Aug. 24, 2026); Resp. to Cert. Pet. 18, Rhoney
v. Barbosa da Cunha, No. 26-104 (Sup. Ct. Aug. 21, 2026); Cert. Pet. 29–32,
Buenrostro-Mendez v. Blanche, No. 26-43 (Sup. Ct. July 2, 2026).

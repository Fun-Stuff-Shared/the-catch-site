Warren Statement on Trump Administration’s Illegitimate Show Trial Into Fed Governor Lisa Cook
Washington, DC – United States Senator Elizabeth Warren, Ranking Member of the Senate Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs Committee, issued the following statement:
“President Trump is convening an illegitimate show trial in his dangerous quest to illegally take over our nation’s central bank. Instead of playing judge in a kangaroo court of his own making, Trump should end the war in Iran and cancel his chaotic tariffs.”

The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Graham). The Senator from Nebraska.
Megaspeed Export Governance and Accountability Act
Mr. RICKETTS. Madam President, I rise today to talk about a pressing
national security issue.
A world where communist China leads AI is bad for America and for the
rest of the world. Right now, the United States is a leader in
developing this technology, and we have the advantage on high-end chips
and high-end chip manufacturing. Communist China knows this, and they
want our technology.
Now, there have been those that have made the argument that communist
China doesn't want or need our chips. However, the recent news about
what is going on with NVIDIA puts a line through this narrative that
communist China indeed does want our chips. Now,
[[Page S5792]]
more than ever, it is important that we maintain our technological
advantage over communist China.
Artificial intelligence--or superintelligence--is how Xi Jinping
thinks he can beat us, and it is so important that we stay in front of
it and not allow communist China to get this technological advantage.
We must use every tool that we have to be able to keep that lead and
make sure that our high-end technology does not end up in communist
China.
That is why I have introduced my Megaspeed Export Governance and
Accountability Act. What this act would do is require the Department of
Commerce to make a decision within the next 30 days on Megaspeed and
whether or not they should be put on the Entity List and banned from
buying our high-end chips. It would have a presumption of denial that
goes along with this, and then it would also require the Department of
Commerce to let us know, in Congress, what decision they made and why
they made it, what was their thought process, why did they deny it or
why did they put Megaspeed on the Entity List.
This is incredibly important because what we have seen is that media
has reported that NVIDIA has sold billions of dollars in chips to
Megaspeed and that these chips have been diverted to communist China.
These concerning ties to communist China must be investigated, and we
must take action right away.
In late 2024, the Department of Commerce began investigating
Megaspeed and whether or not export control should be put on Megaspeed.
The DOJ is also conducting its own separate investigation, which is a
criminal investigation. And what we know so far is that, in 2025,
NVIDIA booked $104 billion in revenue through its Singapore subsidiary.
This was up 72 percent from the previous year.
In June of 2025, NVIDIA put Megaspeed on its internal no-sell list.
NVIDIA's inventory checks reportedly failed to account for about 50,000
chips sold to Megaspeed through partners. Reports are estimating that
these chips are worth anywhere between $7.5 billion and $12 billion.
U.S. officials rightly suspect that Megaspeed has been diverting these
chips to communist China.
The Department of Commerce uses the Entity List to restrict access to
sensitive American technology. Listed companies face additional
licensing requirements on exports, reexports, and transfers of these
controlled items. Listed companies are ones that we suspect may be
having connections to our chief adversaries in the world, like
communist China.
The White House and intelligence officials have pressed Commerce to
restrict Megaspeed. The Departments of State, War, Energy, Treasury,
and the U.S. Trade Representative have also recommended that Megaspeed
be added to the Entity List. Yet the Department of Commerce has not
done this.
My MEGA Act would force this decision from the Department of Commerce
on listing Megaspeed while restricting the transactions on Megaspeed
during the review. It would immediately place a temporary export
restriction on Megaspeed International and require the Department of
Commerce to make a determination within 30 days on whether the company
was added to the Entity List and require Commerce to explain its
decision to Congress, keeping interim restrictions in place until a
final decision takes effect.
Megaspeed's ties to communist China demand action. We know that Alice
Huang, who was related to Megaspeed, has been someone whom our
intelligence officials think is a Chinese spy. We know that her husband
has been responsible for creating lots of data centers in communist
China. These ties should concern everybody who is responsible for
national security in the United States. We must be vigilant against
communist China and what they are doing to steal our technology.
The Department of Commerce should make a timely decision on Megaspeed
and add it to the Entity List, if it deserves being added there.
We need to know what the process is that they are thinking on, what
their philosophy has been, why this hasn't been done already. And if
they are not going to do it, why they are not going to do it.
My bill would make sure this happens. We cannot delay any longer on
Megaspeed. We need immediate action.

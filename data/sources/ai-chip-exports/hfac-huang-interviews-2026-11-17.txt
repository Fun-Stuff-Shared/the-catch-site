Jensen Huang and Nvidia Agree to Appear Before Congress | Republican Foreign Affairs Committee

Washington, D.C. – Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford released a statement after Jensen Huang and NVIDIA General Counsel Tim Teter voluntarily agreed to appear before the House Foreign Affairs Committee, the Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, and the House Intelligence Committee for transcribed interviews.
“We look forward to hearing from Jensen Huang and Tim Teter on their roles in a potential breach of our national security,” said Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford. “The American people need answers on Mr. Huang and Nvidia’s role in allowing dangerous PRC actors to access our most advanced chips, as well as the role of a potential Chinese intelligence asset in this scandal.”
Jensen Huang will appear before the committees on December 1st, and NVIDIA General Counsel will appear on December 2nd, both for transcribed interviews.

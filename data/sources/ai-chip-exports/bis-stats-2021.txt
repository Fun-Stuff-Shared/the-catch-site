Summary

  In 2021, both U.S. exports and imports increased despite of Covid-19 Pandemic. In 2021, U.S. exports
  totaled $1.8 trillion, a 23.1% ($329.0 billion) increase from $1.4 trillion in 2020; U.S. imports totaled
  $2.8 trillion, a 21.3% ($496.9 billion) increase; and the trade deficit was $1.1 trillion (rounded) at a
  record high, a 18.4% ($167.9 billion) increase.

  In 2021, of the $1.8 trillion in U.S. exports, the top commodity sectors were Machinery and Mechanical
  Appliances (22.5%), followed by Chemicals, Plastics, Rubber, and Leather products (17.3%), the same
  as 2020. Oils, Minerals, Lime, and Cement (14.3%) replaced Transportation equipment as the 3 rd top
  commodity sector.

  In 2021, of the $2.8 trillion in U.S. imports, the top commodity sectors were Machinery and Mechanical
  Appliances (29.1%), followed by Chemicals, Plastics, Rubber, and Leather products (14.7%), and
  Transportation Equipment (10.9%), the same as 2020.

  In 2021, U.S. exported $1.8 trillion in goods, of which $39.0 billion (2.8% of the total exports) was
  exported under a government license, $51.4 billion (2.9%) was exported under a government license
  exception/exemption/agreement, and $176.6 billion (10.1%) was exported under No License Required
  with an ECCN reported, which includes 600-Series .y subparagraph items and 0A501 .y subparagraph
  firearms items (EAR99 excluded).

  In 2021, BIS reviewed 40,843 license applications with a total value of $1.4 trillion. Of these, 942 were
  Deemed Export applications and 39,901 were applications for the export and re-export of tangible items,

  In 2021, for tangible items, software and technology, approved licenses totaled 34,369, an increase of 4.8%
  (1,560 more) and the approval rate was 86.1%. Denied license applications totaled 826, an increase of

  mainly due to the decrease in applications from Chinese and Iranian nationals. The approved licenses for

Meeks Delivers Remarks During HFAC Disclosure of BIS Licenses of NVIDIA & AMD Chips to China | House Foreign Affairs Committee

Washington, DC — Representative Gregory W. Meeks, Ranking Member of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, delivered the following remarks, as prepared, during a Business Meeting in which the Committee approved by unanimous consent the disclosure of license information from the Bureau of Industry and Security related to the sale of Nvidia H200 and equivalent AMD chips to China.
“Over the past month, we’ve learned the administration’s handling of chip export controls has put our national security and technology edge at risk.  The Bureau of Industry and Security has failed to enforce the rules, and Congress must now conduct rigorous oversight of the administration’s policies.
“The license information we are disclosing today will help inform the American public about how BIS has handled licenses for Nvidia H200 and equivalent AMD chips to China. The public has the right to know how these decisions were made, and releasing this information makes certain that everyone is reviewing the same set of facts.
“The record shows a troubling contradiction. Even as there were massive delays in license reviews at BIS, the agency seemed to approve licenses for advanced chips to China with little scrutiny.  I have raised questions about this for over a year, and we need to know why.
“In the weeks and months ahead, this committee will find out. That must include public hearings with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and BIS Undersecretary Jeffrey Kessler.”

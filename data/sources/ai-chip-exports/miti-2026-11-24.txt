  THE ADDITION OF MALAYSIAN ENTITIES TO THE US ENTITY LIST - UNDER
    BUREAU OF INDUSTRY AND SECURITY OF THE US DEPARTMENT OF
                           COMMERCE


The Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) refers to the final rule issued by
the United States Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS)
on 23 November 2026, adding seven (7) entities to the US Entity List. (https://public
inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23999.pdf)

There are two (2) entities listed under Malaysia, namely Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd and
MSFusion Sdn. Bhd. Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd is a wholly owned subsidiary of
Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd incorporated in Singapore.

Based on MIDA’s records, PT Megaspeed International is not an entity in Malaysia
nor is it a data centre operator or data centre tenant operating in Malaysia.

MIDA will be issuing a notice to all data centre operators in Malaysia informing them
of the listing, and advising them not to host, lease space to, provide services to the
seven (7) listed entities.

Malaysia remains committed to a secure, transparent and rules-based trading
environment and will not allow itself to be used as a channel to circumvent export
controls. Action will be taken against parties found to have contravened any laws.



MINISTRY OF INVESTMENT, TRADE AND INDUSTRY (MITI)

Chairman Brian Mast Exercises Legislative Authority to Publicly Release AI Chip License Information for China | Republican Foreign Affairs Committee

WASHINGTON, D.C. - This afternoon, Chairman Brian Mast exercised his authorities under the Export Control Reform Act to publicly reveal export control license application information regarding NVIDIA and AMD's AI chip licenses for the People's Republic of China.
The findings include requested sales of 3.4 million AI chips to China, and the approval of more than 879,000 by BIS-including approvals for AI chip exports to Department of War-designated Chinese Military Companies like Alibaba and Tencent. It also includes requests by NVIDIA to depend on possible chip-smuggling fronts to facilitate their sales of advanced AI chips to Chinese companies. This is the most detailed public disclosure in the history of the Export Control Reform Act.

Chairman Mast Sends Letter to Commerce Department on Megaspeed | Republican Foreign Affairs Committee

November 17, 2026
Washington, D.C. - House Foreign Affairs Committee Chairman Brian Mast today sent a letter demanding information from Under Secretary of Commerce Jeffrey Kessler regarding chip smuggling and export licenses for investigations into Megaspeed and its affiliate Speedmatrix.
"More and more reports are coming to light of possibly extraordinary levels of chip smuggling conducted by Megaspeed and its broader corporate network, all under Jensen Huang's nose. We need to get to the bottom of how many advanced AI chips the Chinese Communist Party has gotten its hands on", Chairman Mast said.

         33.   This statement is false because neither President Trump nor his father has

         34.   This statement is also false because neither President Trump nor his

  father has ever been charged with—much less convicted of—any crime for fraud. It is

  well-established that Americans are entitled to file their taxes in the legal manner that

  results in them paying the lowest amount of taxes. See, e.g., Helvering v. Gregory, 69

  F.2d 809, 810 (2d Cir. 1934) (“We agree with the Board and the taxpayer that a

  transaction, otherwise within an exception of the tax law, does not lose its immunity,

  because it is actuated by a desire to avoid, or, if one choose, to evade, taxation. Any

  one may so arrange his affairs that his taxes shall be as low as possible; he is not bound

  to choose that pattern which will best pay the Treasury; there is not even a patriotic

  duty to increase one's taxes.”) (L. Hand, J.), aff’d 293 U.S. 465 (1935) (“The legal right

  of a taxpayer to decrease the amount of what otherwise would be his taxes, or

  altogether avoid them, by means which the law permits, cannot be doubted.”).

         35.   In fact, years before the Book was published, the New York Times, along

  with Defendants Craig and Buettner, has admitted: “The line between legal tax

  avoidance and illegal tax evasion is often murky . . . . There is no shortage of clever

         David Barstow, Susanne Craig and Russ Buettner, Trump Engaged in Suspect

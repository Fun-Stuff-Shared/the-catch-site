How many tickets can I buy?
There is a six (6) ticket limit for per customer, per show.

---

<!-- page 6 -->
This restriction is in place to give as many people as possible a fair chance to buy tickets.
Customers who exceed the published ticket limits may have their tickets cancelled without notice.
Please note: any transactions that are detected as bot purchases, patrons creating duplicate accounts
in order to purchase more than the ticket limit or any other action that indicates a suspicious purchase
may be cancelled without notice.

How much are tickets?
There are a wide range of ticket categories and prices available.

Please refer to the relevant show date you are purchasing for on the Official Ticketing Company’s
site for all ticket categories and prices.

Additional fees relating to the transaction, handling and delivery of tickets may be applied by the
Official Ticketing Company.

How will I receive my ticket(s)?
Tickets can only be accessed via the iOS or Android App for the Official Ticketing Company
associated with the applicable Venue. Make sure you install the relevant Official Ticketing Company’s
App to access tickets.

We recommend you download and install the relevant Official Ticketing Company’s App before
purchasing your ticket(s) but it can still be done after purchase. You will be able to view your ticket
purchase with show date, venue and seat allocation in the relevant App.

I can't find my confirmation email
First, check your email junk or spam folder. If your ticket confirmation email is not in your junk or
spam folder, please contact the Official Ticketing Company’s customer support team:

Ticketmaster customer support // SeatGeek customer support

I’ve changed my mind, can I get a refund or exchange my ticket(s)?
All sales are final. If you change your mind about your ticket purchase, you are not entitled to a
refund or exchange on your ticket purchase.

I have a question about my ticket(s) or purchase, who should I contact?
Please direct all ticket or purchase inquiries to the corresponding Official Ticketing Company’s
customer support team: Ticketmaster customer support // SeatGeek customer support


What if I have an issue with my ticket(s) at the venue?
If you have any issues with your ticket(s) at the venue including if your phone is lost or stolen and
you cannot access your tickets another way, please head to the box office of your venue.

Ensure you bring valid photo ID in order for the venue staff to assist you.

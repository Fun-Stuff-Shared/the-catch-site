Slide 1
We have made the decision to depart as Ed Sheeran's band on the remaining US Loop tour, following the silencing of Macklemore by zionist lobbies. We are a traditional Irish band who never saw ourselves playing in stadiums of that size and that opportunity is not lost on us. We have been friends with Ed for over 10 years now and will continue to be. We believe in dialogue as a means of progressing the plight of the Palestinian people.

Slide 2
It has been a dream to play these shows, but we will not play in venues that silence artists for saying Free Palestine. We encourage everyone to research Robert Kraft, his relationship with Israel and the Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions movement. We witnessed Macklemore's performance and supported it, as did the vast majority of the audience.

Slide 3
We are Irish people who understand colonialism. As founding members of the Irish Artists For Palestine movement, we are committed activists for justice and will continue to be. This whole episode is once again serving to distract from the real issue, over 1000 Palestinians have been killed since the most recent so-called ceasefire. Up the workers. Free Palestine. BEOGA

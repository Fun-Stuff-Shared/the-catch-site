Ed Sheeran: LOOP Tour

ED SHEERAN: LOOP TOUR

Ed Sheeran will return to Gillette Stadium on September 25 and 26, 2026 as part of his LOOP Tour. Sheeran will return to the home of the Patriots and Revolution for his eighth and ninth overall performances at the venue, and the first time since 2023, when he set the venue’s single-show attendance record that still stands today.

The LOOP Tour promises an all-new set design with new songs from Sheeran’s recently released album, Play, as well as fan favorites and some classics added in. It comes on the heels of his most recent Mathematics tour that smashed attendance records all around the globe before wrapping earlier this year. An artist who is constantly evolving, Play finds Sheeran exploring new musical ground through collaboration with producers and musicians from across the globe, as well as diving deeper into the timeless sounds and themes that have made him one of the world’s best-loved pop artists. For more information, visit EdSheeran.com.

Tickets

The Ticket Office is located in the Patriot Place Plaza, outside of the Ticketmaster gate at the north end of the stadium. The Gillette Stadium Ticket Office is open on event days only and will open with parking lots.

  • All tickets will be available via mobile ticketing only. Download the Gillette Stadium App. Tap the “Tickets” icon at the bottom of the screen. Tap the “Access Your Tickets” graphic.
  • Sign in using your email address and password associated with your Account Manager if you have not already done so.
  • Once you are signed in, the app will show a listing of your purchased tickets for upcoming events and games. Select the ticket for the event you are attending.
  • Tap “VIEW BARCODE” to display the barcode to present at the stadium gates. Be sure to have this page open when you approach the gate as you make your way through security. Alternatively, you can add the tickets to your mobile wallet for easy access entering the gates.
Bag Policy
  • Clear Bag Policy:
    • Non-clear bags are not to exceed 4.5” x 6.5”.
    • Clear bags are not to exceed 12” x 6” x 12”.
  • Banner Information:
    • Banners larger than 11” x 17” are not permitted.
    • Lights/battery packs are not permitted on signage, clothing or any other objects.
    • Stadium/artist management reserves the right to prohibit signs or banners due to message content.
  • Electronics Information:
    • No GoPro, video cameras, tripods or monopods.
    • No professional cameras or cameras with detachable lenses.
    • Polaroid cameras and portable phone chargers are permitted.
  • Selfie sticks, backpacks, lasers, umbrellas, and outside food and beverage are NOT permitted inside the stadium. For a full list of permitted and prohibited items, please click here.

Parking

PREPAID PARKING

As a ticket holder, you can save time on event day by purchasing a prepaid parking pass. This option is the ONLY way to park on the stadium side of Route 1.

A spot in one of our stadium-side lots is guaranteed regardless of when you arrive. In order to purchase prepaid parking, you must use the email address associated with your concert tickets. You may only purchase one (1) pass per account per event.

Please note that parking passes are limited and are subject to availability.

GENERAL PARKING

Gillette Stadium also offers general parking on the opposite side of Route 1. General parking is included in the price of your ticket.

Click here to see a parking map.

ADA PARKING

Enter via P1, if traveling from the north, or enter via P7, if traveling from the south.
Click here to see a parking map. You will be directed to your parking spot upon entry.
MBTA

There will be MBTA service from BOS only.

The Boston special event train will depart from South Station with stops at Back Bay and Dedham Corporate Center before arriving at Foxboro Station.

  • Depart South Station: 2:40 PM
  • Depart Back Bay: 2:45 PM
  • Depart Dedham Corporate Center: 3:00 PM
  • Arrive at Foxboro Station: 3:40 PM
 
Note: The train will depart Foxboro Station 30 minutes after the concert ends. 
 

Roundtrip tickets are $20 each and will go on sale at 11:00 AM on Friday, September 11 exclusively via the mTicket app.

Please note: This is a special event train and ticket sales will be stopped once the maximum capacity is reached. Regular Commuter Rail tickets and passes are not valid for special event trains. Guests choosing to travel to Gillette Stadium for the game via the Foxboro Station weekday Commuter Rail service will not be able to return to Boston following the conclusion of the game via the special event train without purchasing a corresponding special event train ticket. The last Foxboro Station weekday Commuter Rail train departs for Boston at 10:49 p.m., while the special event train will depart 30 minutes following the conclusion of the concert. For more information, visit www.mbta.com/destinations/gillette-stadium.

Pick up and Drop off

Gillette Stadium has a designated rideshare drop off and pick up location in Lot 16 (adjacent to Bass Pro Shops) for Uber and Lyft users. Fans must schedule an Uber or Lyft ride to and from the venue through their respective mobile apps in order to use those services.

For the shortest route to Lot 16 after the event, exit the stadium via the CVS Health gate and follow the pedestrian path (see below).

  • For concerts and other events, taxi pickup is in Lot 5B.
  • Visit our Parking Page to view all parking maps.
 

Please note that Gillette Stadium makes no representation about the availability, cost, or quality of any rideshare, taxi or limousine service. The companies and individuals that operate these services are solely responsible for providing these services. Gillette Stadium suggests that you contact these companies and individuals in advance of an event to confirm that the services will be available or have a secondary plan for a safe ride home.

ADA Information
  • Gillette Stadium provides two (2) dedicated ADA entrances for guests needing additional accommodations and requiring elevator access: one on the west side of the stadium at the Bank of America Gate and W2, and one on the east side of the stadium at the Ticketmaster Gate. To see a map of these stadium entrances, please click here.
  • Please visit our Accessible Services page to learn about Accessible Seating and Relocation or call our Ticket Office at (800) 543-1776.
Family Restrooms

All public restrooms are accessible to guests with ADA needs. There is also one (1) changing table located in every restroom in the stadium.

  • 100 Level: Four (4) family restrooms behind section 138.
  • 200 Level: Two (2) family restrooms behind sections 216 and 224.
    • Cross Insurance Club: One (1) in NE, (1) one in NW, one (1) in (SE) and one (1) in SW – only open to customers with Cross Insurance Club tickets
    • Optum Field Lounge: one (1) family restroom—only open to customers with OFL tickets
    • Sports Illustrated Pavilion: one (1) family restroom—only open to customers with Sports Illustrated Pavilion Access
    • G-P Atrium: Four (4) family restrooms in total—only open to customers with G-P Atrium tickets
  • 300 Level: Four (4) family restrooms behind sections 304, 314, 326, 336, and two (2) family restrooms by Section 300 in the North End Zone (on either side of lighthouse).
Contactless and Cashless Payments Only

Gillette Stadium is a completely cashless stadium.

Only contactless payment options will be accepted within the stadium. Event attendees will be able to utilize one of our new Cash-to-Card kiosks to convert cash to Visa® prepaid cards. Visa® prepaid cards will be valid anywhere Visa® is accepted. For more information on our Cash-to-Card kiosks, click here.

Waze

Fans who use local roads will experience delays in arrival time due to road closures that are not captured by traffic and navigation apps.

When driving to Gillette Stadium, we recommend you use Waze for navigation services and traffic updates.

Reminder: access to Gillette Stadium on event day is via Route 1 only.

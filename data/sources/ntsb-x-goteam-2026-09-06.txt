NTSB has launched a go team to investigate Sunday's runway excursion accident involving a B-767-300 cargo airplane at Miami International Airport. The airplane was operated as 21 Air Flight 7598 from San Juan, Puerto Rico, to Miami, Florida. NTSB Chairwoman Jennifer Homendy will be the board member on scene.
A media briefing in Miami will be scheduled for Monday. The briefing time and location will be announced on this X feed on Monday.

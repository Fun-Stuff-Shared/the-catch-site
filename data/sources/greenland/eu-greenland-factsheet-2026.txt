EU-Greenland Partnership
SEPTEMBER 2026




The EU and Greenland have a long-standing partnership built on shared priorities. Through the
new EU-Greenland Joint Declaration our partnership is evolving to respond to a changing context
and new opportunities.
Building on strong foundations in areas such as education and fisheries, it is now expanding
into new areas that reflect Greenland’s needs and priorities. Through a new €200 million Global
Gateway Partnership Package, we will boost support for connectivity, sustainable energy and
critical raw materials.
Together, the EU and Greenland are creating opportunities for people, supporting sustainable
development and economic resilience while strengthening cooperation in the Arctic.

    Sustainable Raw Materials
            The EU and Greenland cooperate to develop sustainable raw
            materials value chains that support Greenland’s economic
            diversification and contribute to a resilient European
            industry. The GreenRoc’s Amitsoq graphite project and
            the Malmbjerg Molybdenum mining project are concrete
            examples of the potential for integrated Greenland–EU value
            chains.

                     We are promoting sustainable fisheries management

FOREIGN NATIONALS
Greenland´s international twist is growing, now representing 4.5
per cent of population total. The largest immigrant groups are
Philippines, Thai and Sri Lankans. In 2014, 15,521 Greenlandic
born lived in Denmark. In 2024, the number has risen to 17,287.

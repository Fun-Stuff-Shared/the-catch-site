STATEMENT: Counsel Responds After Trump Administration Wrongly Charges Former FBI Intelligence Analyst Nikki Floris | Washington Litigation Group

WASHINGTON, D.C., October 23, 2026 — Today, the Department of Justice charged Nikki Floris, a former Deputy Assistant Director of the FBI’s Counterintelligence Division, following a months-long campaign by President Donald Trump and his allies falsely accusing her of treason. In response, her counsel—including Margaret Donovan and Chris Mattei from Koskoff, Koskoff & Bieder and Mary Dohrmann and Kyle R. Freeny of Washington Litigation Group—issued the following statement:
“Nikki Floris is a patriot. She served our nation as a dedicated FBI intelligence officer for nearly 20 years. Yet today, the DOJ has accused her falsely of a crime on the say-so of politicians, not on the facts. It’s no coincidence the government is filing these nonsense charges now, years later, on the eve of an election.
“The government’s reasons for charging Nikki are as transparent as they are unjust: to push a false narrative for a shot at political points. It’s another sad day for the DOJ, but we look forward to Nikki’s day in court.”

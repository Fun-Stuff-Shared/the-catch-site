                                 STATEMENT OF FACTS

INTRODUCTION

  1. This affidavit concerns materially false statements made by Nikki FLORIS and Bradley

     BENAVIDES to the U.S. House of Representatives Committee on the Judiciary in

     connection with an authorized Congressional investigation into, among other things, the

     Foreign Influence Task Force’s (FITF’s) involvement in the FBI’s various investigations

     into the then-President’s son, Hunter Biden.       During the Committee’s investigation,

     Congressional investigators sought information concerning FLORIS’s and BENAVIDES’s

     knowledge of and involvement in the investigations into Hunter Biden. On July 19, 2023,

     FLORIS, and on September 28, 2023, BENAVIDES, knowingly and willfully provided

     materially false information about their awareness of and involvement in the FBI’s

     investigations into Hunter Biden. As detailed below, those statements were contradicted by

     documentary evidence, communications, and witness accounts and concerned matters

     directly relevant to the congressional investigation.

  2. The evidence further establishes probable cause to believe that FLORIS and BENAVIDES

     made these statements for the purpose of concealing FITF’s engagement with and

     communications about the Hunter Biden investigations and impeding the congressional

     investigation. Accordingly, there is probable cause to believe that FLORIS and

     BENAVIDES violated 18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2), by knowingly and willfully making

     materially false statements in a matter within the jurisdiction of the legislative branch.

FLORIS’S AND BENAVIDES’S ENGAGEMENT WITH THE HUNTER BIDEN
INVESTIGATIONS

  6. FLORIS and BENAVIDES are former FBI officials. FLORIS served as the Deputy

     Assistant Director and functional head of FITF from mid-2019 through mid-2022.

     BENAVIDES served as the Section Chief of FITF from mid-2019 through the 2020

     election. Although FITF was originally focused on combating foreign influence by Russia,

     its scope expanded to include foreign influence by other nation-state actors. As FITF’s

     scope expanded in the leadup to the 2020 election, so did its size. In the beginning, FITF

     was a small task force focused on Russia. In mid-2019, it became a standalone section

     with its own section chief—BENAVIDES. FITF—led by FLORIS and BENAVIDES—

     was tasked by the FBI with securing the 2020 election from foreign malign actors. FITF

     was disbanded in 2025.

     FLORIS and BENAVIDES were active participants. In October 2020, both FLORIS and

     BENAVIDES worked on the FBI’s internal and Congressional responses to reporting on

     the FBI’s receipt of Hunter Biden’s laptop, with FLORIS receiving, discussing, and passing

     along updates on the investigations through late 2021.

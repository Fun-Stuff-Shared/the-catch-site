        The Committee on the Judiciary is conducting oversight of the Federal Bureau of
Investigation’s (FBI) operation, codenamed Round River, during the 2020 presidential election.
Recently released information reveals that the operation categorized prominent Republicans and
conservative organizations as “conduits” of Russian disinformation, while certain Democrats
such as President Joe Biden and his son, Hunter Biden, were “targets” of such disinformation.1 In
response to this information, you issued a statement suggesting that Round River was used as
political lawfare to suppress evidence tying the Biden family to corruptive business dealings with
Burisma, a Ukrainian energy company, rather than offering a complete picture of potential
foreign threats.2 We appreciate your continued transparency, and we write to request additional
information regarding Round River.

       According to public reporting, the FBI’s Foreign Influence Task Force (FITF), which was
based at FBI Headquarters, initiated Round River to address “malign foreign influence” during
the 2020 presidential election.3 Round River extended to the FBI’s Pittsburgh Field Office to
allegedly disrupt an investigation led by the U.S. Attorney’s Office that tied the Biden family to
corrupt business dealings in Ukraine.4 On August 12, 2026, President Donald Trump’s
Government Transparency Task Force released a declassified document from the Office of the

Director of National Intelligence, listing over 70 organizations and individuals, both domestic
and foreign, that were associated with the “2019 impeachment proceedings of President Trump”
or the “Biden family Ukraine scandal.”5 Specifically, the document categorized certain
Democrats, including President Joe Biden, as “targets” of Russian disinformation, and other
notable Republicans as “conduits” of such disinformation.6 On her first day in office, former

abuses of prosecutorial discretion.’7

        According to your office, as a part of the Round River operation, the FBI recruited 53
confidential human sources (CHS), and of these sources, 14 of them provided “derogatory
information on [President] Joe Biden.”8 Subsequent reporting indicates that one CHS expressed
concerns to U.S. intelligence officials about Burisma hiring Hunter Biden as early as 2015 while
Joe Biden served as Vice President, and another CHS reported that the Biden-Burisma
connection was “part of a larger corruption scheme in Ukraine where Burisma purportedly paid
figures through fake contracts.”9 A third CHS who had direct contact with Hunter Biden and
discussions about Burisma corruption presented his information to intelligence officials during
the 2019 partisan Democrat impeachment investigation, which concerned allegations of Biden
influence peddling in Ukraine.10

        We also appreciate that the FBI is examining how Round River affected congressional
oversight of the Wray FBI and the FITF. You have stated that the FBI is “investigating whether
the FITF used information learned from the Round River assessment to undermine or influence
ongoing investigations by only focusing on the subset of CHS involving the Bidens.”11
Specifically, you highlighted that some of the defensive briefings to Congress during the 2020
election “may have been overly focused on just Biden and Ukraine rather than the balanced
picture of all foreign threats.”12

        In light of this new information, you stated that the Bureau has “identified tens of
thousands of responsive pages” of documents.13 Accordingly, to advance our oversight, we
respectfully request that the FBI produce the following documents and information:

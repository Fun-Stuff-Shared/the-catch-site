White House Transparency Task Force,

Thank you for providing the documents from other intelligence community components
regarding the Round River assessment. We are working diligently with the Department of
Justice to compile and provide the task force with the full body of evidence within our own
agency related to this matter. To date, we have proactively identified tens of thousands of
responsive pages and are preparing them for production for you and Congress.

We share the concerns raised in the materials you transmitted suggesting the FBI’s thenForeign Influence Task Force (FITF), embedded in the FBI Counterintelligence Division at
Headquarters, may have inappropriately focused on a small subset of information gleaned from
the Round River assessment. As you know, Round River identified at least 53 Confidential
Human Sources (CHS) whose identities were potentially known to foreign intelligence and
under hostile control. At least 14 of these CHSs reported derogatory information on Joe Biden.
At my direction, this FBI will take the unprecedented step of providing you with the source
reporting from all 53 sources so you can finally see the facts.

As you are aware, I immediately disbanded the FITF upon assuming the role of FBI Director.
While our internal review of the FITF is not yet complete, we are investigating whether the FITF
used information learned from the Round River assessment to undermine or influence ongoing
investigations by only focusing on the subset of CHS involving the Bidens. We remain concerned
that defensive briefings to Congress may have been overly focused on just Biden and Ukraine
rather than the balanced picture of all foreign threats.

Although these alleged activities occurred during a different era within the Bureau, my team is
firmly committed to identifying, investigating, and holding accountable all abuses. We are
equally committed to ensuring that future counterintelligence operations remain focused
exclusively on legitimate foreign threats and are never used to advance political narratives.

Kash Patel
Director

Public Notice: Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, Middletown, Iowa | US EPA

Summary
Public Notice of Emergency Permit
In order to continue to address an imminent and substantial endangerment, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Region 7, 11201 Renner Blvd., Lenexa, KS 66219, has issued an emergency permit to store and treat hazardous waste by intentional detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP), in a location not covered by the effective Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) permit.
Emergency Permit Permittee: United States Department of the Army
Location of Permitted Facility: Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, 17571 Des Moines County Highway 79, Middletown, IA 52638-5000
EPA RCRA ID No.: IA7213820445
Description of Wastes: Lead azide hazardous waste that exhibits the characteristic of reactivity (energetic) and exhibits the characteristic of toxicity for lead.
Activity Authorized by Emergency Permit: EPA issued an emergency permit that became effective on November 13, 2026, for the storage and treatment of lead azide hazardous waste by in-place, intentional detonation at a location not covered by the existing effective RCRA permit.
Reason Emergency Permit is Necessary: IAAAP’s existing RCRA permit authorizes treatment, storage, and disposal of hazardous waste in hazardous waste management units at the facility. However, the storage and treatment of this lead azide hazardous waste is in a location not covered for treatment, storage, or disposal under the effective RCRA permit. The recrystallization of the stored lead azide has increased the material’s sensitivity to friction and shock, and disturbing the containers of lead azide could create an unacceptable risk of unintentional detonation. The Army’s evaluation of a range of potential alternatives for the treatment of lead azide culminated in the secretary of the Army selecting in-place, controlled, intentional detonation for the treatment of lead azide.
This emergency permit is required to protect human health and the environment; ensure adherence of safe storage and treatment procedures; and authorize the continuation of storage and intentional treatment of this hazardous waste by in-place detonation in order to safely manage the explosive hazard and mitigate the imminent threat to the surrounding community and the environment. This emergency permit requires the collection of samples and monitoring (pre-detonation, during detonation, and post-detonation), pre- and post-detonation public notifications, implementation of cleanup, and reporting requirements.
Date Emergency Permit Issued: November 13, 2026
Permit Effective Dates: November 13, 2026, through February 11, 2027
A separate notice will be issued when the Administrative Record is available for public review. For more information or to request a copy of the Emergency Permit for Storage and Treatment of Lead Azide, please contact Patricia Murrow, EPA project manager, at 913-551-7627; or Patrick Hurley, EPA community involvement coordinator, at 913-551-7202.

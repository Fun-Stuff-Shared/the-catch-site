     MOTION TO SUPPLEMENT EMERGENCY MOTION FOR STAY
        PENDING DISPOSITION OF PETITION FOR REVIEW

      Petitioners Iowa Environmental Council and Sierra Club, by and through

undersigned counsel, respectfully move this Court for leave to file additional

declarations in support of their Emergency Motion for Stay Pending Disposition of

Petition for Review. Specifically, Petitioners seek leave to file the following

affidavit and declarations, attached as Exhibit A:

          1. Affidavit of Mark J. Thomas, PhD

          2. Declaration of Dr. Ranajit Sahu, PhD

          3. Declaration of Geoff Fruin, City Manager of the City of Iowa City
                                          1
      The declarations include factual attestations that support arguments made in

the Motion for Stay, including: the inadequacy of public notice and information;

challenges by subject matter experts to the accuracy of scientific claims relied

upon by the Army and the Environmental Protection Agency, including whether a

delay to allow judicial review would materially increase the hazard posed by the

stored lead azide; and the lack of data to show that the permit conditions are

adequate to protect human health and the environment from releases of hazardous

waste constituents.

      The declarations were unavailable when Petitioners filed the Emergency

Motion, partly because of its emergency nature and partly because some respond to

events that occurred after that filing. Undersigned counsel submits them as soon as

practicable and before Respondents’ opposition to Petitioners’ Stay Motion has

been filed. The declarations are material to the underlying matter, and Respondents

will not be prejudiced because they corroborate facts and arguments already before

the Court.

      For the foregoing reasons, Petitioners respectfully request that the Court

accept for filing the declarations attached as Exhibit A, and that they be considered

as supplemental support for Petitioners’ emergency motion.

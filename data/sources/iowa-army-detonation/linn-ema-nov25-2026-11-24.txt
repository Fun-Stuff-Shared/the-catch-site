Public Information Update: U.S. Army to Detonate Lead Azide at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant November 25 - News & Notices - Linn County Emergency Management

Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 3:01 PM

Linn County, Iowa – November 24, 2026 – The U.S. Army announced it plans to conduct a controlled detonation of unstable, degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP) located in Middletown, Iowa, on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at approximately 9 a.m. The Army has stated it will announce a more precise time after evaluating changing weather patterns and waiting for the safest possible conditions.

Projected Area of Impact

The Army released updated mapping models on Tuesday, November 24 showing the projected area of impact is southwest of the lead detonation site. Linn County is not in the projected plume area.

Models are subject to change based on actual weather conditions on the day of the detonation. Even with variations between forecast models and actual conditions, Linn County is expected to remain outside the area of impact.

The Iowa Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) is recommending precautionary actions for people and animals in the projected area of impact only. This means if you are in Linn County on the day of the detonation – or any area outside of the projected area of impact – according to current weather forecasts and HHS advisories, you do not need to take protective actions related to the detonation.

If you are traveling for the Thanksgiving holiday, please check the Army’s website for the most up-to-date information and conditions.

Future Updates

The Linn County Emergency Management Agency (EMA) expects this will be the last joint update from local officials through EMA unless the forecast and the Army’s projections change substantially to include actions needed by Linn County residents or visitors.

Protecting the public’s safety and health continue to be the top priorities.

The Army stated in a press briefing this morning (November 24) that they will continue to provide public updates leading up to the detonation and post-detonation. The public is encouraged to monitor the Army’s website for these updates at https://www.army.mil/asc#org-iowa-army-ammunition-plant.

The public can also find information related to the lead azide detonation on the State of Iowa’s website at https://homelandsecurity.iowa.gov/iowa-army-munitions-disposition-resources.

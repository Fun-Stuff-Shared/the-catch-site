EPA continues to closely monitor for any possible lead levels in the air following this morning’s controlled detonation of lead azide in Middletown, IA. At this point, ALL EPA real-time air monitors continue to report ZERO detection of airborne lead throughout the region.

Our air monitors are collecting data every five minutes, providing us real-time analysis of air quality. EPA’s data can be viewed as it becomes available at: https://t.co/dzvV8yHhay

We pre-positioned 300 EPA employees and contractors, as well as robust equipment capabilities, to collect data as quickly and accurately as possible following this morning’s controlled detonation. Prior to the event, we collected 1,200 air, land, dust, and water samples to be further prepared to confirm any changes in levels compared to pre-detonation.

We are working closely with our federal partners, including @USArmy, @fema, @HHSGov, @USDA to ensure any and all action is being taken to protect human health and the environment. Members of the public looking for information from EPA should call 1(833)-649-9624. We request that this number only be utilized by individuals in the local area.

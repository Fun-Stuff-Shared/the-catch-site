Middletown, Iowa - The controlled detonation at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant is now scheduled on Wednesday, November 25, 2026 to allow for more optimal weather conditions and all mitigation measures to be emplaced.
The Army and our interagency partners have been working around the clock to safeguard all Americans in this region.
Additional details on this situation will be forthcoming.
The health and safety of all Americans has and always will be our first priority.

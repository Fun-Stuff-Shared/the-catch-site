From the official X account of the acting Secretary of the Army, Hon. Adam Telle:

Public safety is the Army’s top priority, and I have directed Army Materiel Command to postpone tomorrow’s controlled detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa until Monday, November 23. This will delay the detonation until the latest possible moment prior to a forecasted temperature drop, which would risk further destabilization of the material.

In March of this year, the Army uncovered degrading Lead Azide housed in bunkers at the installation and immediately began communicating with stakeholders and the public in Middletown and the modeled impact area about the danger it posed. In coordination with the Environmental Protection Agency, experts, and the local community, it was determined that a controlled detonation was the option that posed the lowest risk to the public in the immediate and surrounding area.

Public communication is essential to public trust. Therefore, the Army will continue to actively engage with state and local officials throughout the region to distribute accurate, up-to-date information.

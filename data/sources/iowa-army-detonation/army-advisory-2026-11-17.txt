On Nov. 20 at approximately noon, the United States Army will detonate degraded lead
azide (Pb(N3)2) at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant located at 17571 DMC Highway 79,
Middletown, IA 52638. This detonation has been planned in coordination with the U.S.
Environmental Protection Agency under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act. In-
place detonation by Explosive Ordnance Disposal experts is the safest way to eliminate the
explosive safety hazard. The degraded lead azide has been deemed unsafe for relocation.

Extensive modeling efforts predict that the potential level of lead particles in the air may be
elevated. The detonation will result in an air plume with some lead-containing particles
which will disperse in the air and deposit on the ground. Lead is toxic to humans and
animals. The goal of this advisory is to provide the public with actionable information out of
an abundance of caution to minimize lead exposure. There are two primary pathways of
concern:

   1. Lead exposure by breathing in air after the detonation.
   2. Lead exposure from dust on surfaces.

The US Army will release two maps; one for airborne exposure to lead dust through
respiration, and one for exposure from lead dust on surfaces. The potential airborne
exposure map is current as of today, but it will change prior to the detonation due to
weather forecast changes. The maps will be updated at this website:
https://www.army.mil/asc#org-iowa-army-ammunition-plant. Members of the public
should check for updates to these maps:

    •   48 hours in advance of the detonation.
    •   6 hours in advance of the detonation and every hour thereafter until the detonation.
    •   After the event, the US Army will issue a ﬁnal estimate of the impacted area.

To minimize potential exposure, members of the public who live or work in the affected
areas should consider taking the following actions on the day of the detonation:

   •    Consider avoiding the affected area.
   •    If you are inside of the affected areas, avoid outdoor air by staying indoors.
   •    Keep windows and doors closed.
   •    If it is safe to do so, set heating and cooling systems to “recirculate” to minimize
        outdoor air exposure and maximize indoor air ﬁltration until the plume passes by
        your community.
   •    Avoid unnecessary travel through impacted areas.
   •    After the US Army determines the air exposure period has ended, consider cleaning
        indoor and outdoor surfaces such as play surfaces for children and windowsills.

•   If your car was parked outside, consider washing your car at a car wash to minimize
    your contact with potentially exposed surfaces.
•   Learn more about actions you can take to protect you and your family from potential
    exposures to lead at https://www.epa.gov/lead/actions-reduce-potential-lead-
    exposure and https://www.epa.gov/lead/learn-about-lead.

Deposition of lead-containing dust may occur after the detonation on surfaces
within certain areas affected by the detonation plume,
All humans, but especially children, pregnant individuals, older adults, and other
sensitive populations should avoid outdoor activities within affected areas.
All members of the public who reside or work within this affected area should:
   •   Remain indoors during the potential exposure period if they cannot evacuate
beforehand;
   •   Close all windows, exterior doors, and other openings during the exposure
period;
   •   Turn off or place heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems in
recirculation mode, minimize outdoor air intake, and maximize indoor air
ﬁltration during the potential exposure period; and
   •   Avoid travelling through the affected area during the potential exposure period.
   •   Wet-clean surfaces that may be contacted by children, including outdoor play

equipment, toys, tables and chairs, handrails, patios, decks, exterior windowsills, and other high-
contact surfaces. Such instructions should include speciﬁc cleaning and dust mitigation procedures
to address deposited and airborne lead particles which minimizes overall human exposure to lead.


Media Advisory:
Middletown, Iowa -- Two controlled detonations are scheduled at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant on
Nov. 20 at approximately noon. Residents will be asked to vacate the area from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.
local time, and dust is expected to linger for no more than one hour, depending on wind speed. This
is a planned and controlled safety operation to dispose of the degraded lead azide and has been
permitted by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency under the Resource Conservation and
Recovery Act.While safety measures will be fully enforced, the controlled detonation will cause
temporary noise, ground vibrations, and a dust plume that will contain some lead. Residents in
Middletown will hear a loud noise; feel vibrations from two consecutive, rapid detonations; and will
see a plume of dust. Due to the potential for localized window damage, the U.S. Army recommends
that residents in the Middletown City Park neighborhood temporarily vacate the area during the
detonations out of an abundance of caution. Residents are asked to vacate starting at 8 a.m. when
the explosives team begins setting up the detonations. If residents need a place to stay, they are
invited to come to the Armed Forces Reserve Center at 16815 DMC HWY 34 Middletown, IA
52638. All family members are welcome, including pets. Please ensure pets are appropriately
secure in carrying containers or on leashes. Refreshments will be available at this relatively new
building, complete with a gym and classrooms. It is the same location as the open house the Army
hosted on the detonation. The U.S. Army has taken extraordinary measures to mitigate the plume
by following strict permit requirements set by the EPA such as earthworks, soil wetting, and air
sampling. Pre-controlled detonation sampling has taken place; air monitors have been established
for perimeter air monitoring during the event; and environmental sampling of the blast radius will
take place. MEDIA ADVISORY: You are invited to go to the Armed Forces Reserve Center at
116815 DMC HWY 34 Middletown, IA 52638 to view the detonation.

Earlier this year, the Army publicly disclosed that it had discovered degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown. Since then, the Army has been in conversation with the local community and has held open public forums in Middletown on how to handle it. 

Because the material cannot be relocated and no one can say when it might detonate on its own, causing an uncontrolled explosion, a controlled detonation is the safest and best option.

The detonation is scheduled for Friday, November 20, at approximately noon. I encourage everyone in the area to follow the Army's advisory and check http://army.mil/asc for updated maps.

Public Notice: Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, Middletown, Iowa | US EPA

Summary
Public Notice of Emergency Permit
In order to continue to address an imminent and substantial endangerment, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Region 7, 11201 Renner Blvd., Lenexa, KS 66219, has issued a third emergency permit to store hazardous waste at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP) in a location not covered by their effective Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) permit.
Emergency Permit Permittee: United States Department of the Army
Location of Permitted Facility: Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, 17571 Des Moines County Highway 79, Middletown, IA 52638-5000
EPA RCRA ID No.: IA7213820445
Description of Wastes: Lead azide hazardous waste that exhibits the characteristic of reactivity (energetic) and exhibits the characteristic of toxicity for lead.
Activity Authorized by Emergency Permit: EPA issued an emergency permit that became effective on September 29, 2026, for the storage of lead azide hazardous waste in place at a location not covered by the effective RCRA permit.
Reason Emergency Permit is Necessary: IAAAP’s existing RCRA permit authorizes treatment, storage, and disposal of hazardous waste in hazardous waste management units at the facility. However, the storage of this lead azide hazardous waste is in a location not covered for treatment, storage, or disposal under the effective RCRA permit. The recrystallization of the stored lead azide has increased the material’s sensitivity to friction and shock. This emergency permit is required to protect human health and the environment, ensure adherence of safe storage procedures, and authorize the continuation of storage of this hazardous waste in-place to safely manage the explosive hazard and mitigate the imminent threat to the surrounding community and the environment.
Date Emergency Permit Issued: September 29, 2026
Permit Effective Dates: September 29, 2026, through December 26, 2026

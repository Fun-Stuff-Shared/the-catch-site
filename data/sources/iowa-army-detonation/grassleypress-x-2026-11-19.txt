🚨 PSA: Tomorrow at approximately noon, the @USArmy plans to conduct a controlled detonation of unstable lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown.

On October 15, the Army held a town meeting to speak directly with residents of Middletown and communities within a 50-mile radius regarding the detonation.

The Army has stated that an unplanned explosion of lead azide would be catastrophic and is moving forward with the detonation because it is the safest possible way to handle the unstable lead azide.

The Army has assured Sen. @ChuckGrassley that they are taking appropriate safety precautions to protect Iowans' health. For more information and updates, Iowans can visit the Army's website:
https://www.army.mil/asc#org-iowa-army-ammunition-plant

 21 Air We are devastated by the accident involving one of our aircraft in Miami on 09/06. Our deepest condolences are with the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives. Our immediate priorities are supporting those affected, assisting the authorities, and ensuring that accurate information is communicated as it becomes available. We are cooperating fully with the National Transportation Safety Board, the Federal Aviation Administration, and local authorities. The circumstances of the accident are under investigation. Additional verified information will be posted on this page as it becomes available. 21 Air has established a toll-free Family Assistance Line to provide information directly to families of crew members and personnel involved in this event.

Forecast and Polling Average Update:

With the admission from “Wedgewood Polling” that their purported polls were in fact fabricated, we have removed 17 polls from our race-level polling lists and averages.

As polling and averages are a component of our forecast model, we have rerun the model and all of our forecasts now reflect the removal of the fraudulent data. 

The removal of these polls has not changed the projected seat totals or chamber control probabilities in the Senate or House.

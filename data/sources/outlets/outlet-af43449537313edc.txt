Shri Thanedar (D-Mich.) walks to a vote at the U.S. Capitol, on April 29, 2025. | Francis Chung/POLITICO By Andrew Howard 08/05/2026 09:59 AM EDT Share Copy link X Facebook Email Reddit WhatsApp LinkedIn Michigan Rep. Shri Thanedar lost his primary to democratic socialist Donavan McKinney on Wednesday — the latest victory for progressives who’ve been on a hot streak of ousting incumbents in safe-blue districts. Thanedar’s 13th District was a top priority for groups like Justice Democrats that are committed to primarying incumbent members that they believe aren’t in line with the areas they represent. Justice Democrats helped recruit McKinney into the race and spent nearly $1 million on his behalf, making his campaign one of its biggest targets this cycle. McKinney, a state representative, had secured the backing of a number of progressive House Democrats, including some members of the Congressional Black Caucus who bucked party leadership, which was standing behind Thanedar in the heavily Black Detroit-based district. Some of Thanedar’s colleagues were also frustrated by his efforts to force a vote on impeaching President Donald Trump last year, an effort he ultimately backed away from . The district is a safe seat for Democrats, and McKinney is expected to win easily in November. Related Tags Progressives 2026 Elections POLITICO Forecast Global power is in flux.

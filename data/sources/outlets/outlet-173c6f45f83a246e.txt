Shares for JPMorgan Chase are up on Friday after the biggest U.S. bank by assets reported first quarter profits that beat Wall Street predictions. Net revenue rose 25% to $39.3 billion, including $868 million of net investment securities losses. Gross investment banking revenue was $881 million, up 21%. Meanwhile, average loans went up 6%; average deposits slipped 8%.

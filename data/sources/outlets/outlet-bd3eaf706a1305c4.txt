Ireland Scotland Wales Isle of Man Guernsey Jersey Local News Princess Eugenie gives birth to baby girl in Portugal Image source, PA Media By Ella Kipling Published 4 August 2026, 18:31 BST Updated 4 hours ago Princess Eugenie has given birth to a baby girl at a hospital in Lisbon, Portugal. The baby was born on Monday at 18:20 BST, weighing 6lbs 9oz. This is Princess Eugenie's third child with her husband, Jack Brooksbank, following the births of her sons August Philip Hawke Brooksbank, who was born in 2021, and Ernest George Ronnie Brooksbank, who was born in 2023. A statement from Buckingham Palace read: "Their Majesties The King and Queen and other members of The Royal Family were delighted to be informed of the news." In a post on Instagram, the princess, 36, wrote: "Jack and I are so excited to announce Baby Girl Brooksbank!! "We are beyond in love with our girlie." They are yet to announce a name. The new arrival - who will not be an HRH - will be 15th in line to the throne, moving ahead of their great-uncle the Duke of Edinburgh, Prince Edward. The couple live between Portugal and Ivy Cottage, in the grounds of Kensington Palace. They announced Princess Eugenie's pregnancy in May. She posted a photograph of her young sons holding a baby scan with the caption: "Baby Brooksbank due 2026!" The Palace said on social media at the time: "His Majesty The King has been informed and is delighted with the news." Princess Eugenie expecting third child this summer Published 4 May Eugenie and Brooksbank married in October 2018. The baby will be a fifth grandchild for the princess's father, Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. The former prince was stripped of his royal titles, styles and honours by King Charles last year after further details of his relationship with convicted paedophile Jeffrey Epstein came to light. When Mountbatten-Windsor lost his titles, both princesses kept theirs, but neither is a working royal. Eugenie is a director at the art gallery Hauser & Wirth in Mayfair, and her husband has worked in hospitality and marketing.

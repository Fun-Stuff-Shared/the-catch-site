A 911 dispatcher said she could hear a "woman screaming and crying" before TikTok influencer Sara Gilson was allegedly killed by her husband, shortly after she outed him as a suspected pedophile on social media. Gilson, who also went by Sara Duffey, posted several weeks ago accusing her husband of being a pedophile. On July 23, Owasso, Oklahoma, police responding to a reported domestic violence incident found both Sara Duffey and Jeremiah Shawn Duffey dead. Authorities are investigating the deaths as a murder-suicide . "I’m trying to reach him back, but I could hear a female in the background screaming and crying," the dispatcher could be heard saying, according to dispatch audio obtained by Fox News Digital.

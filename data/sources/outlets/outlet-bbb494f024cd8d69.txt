Photograph: Martin Rickett/PA UK news England striker Ivan Toney charged with assault after Soho nightclub incident Met police say charge relates to alleged assault that reportedly took place in December at venue in London PA Media Fri 7 Aug 2026 05.40 EDT Last modified on Fri 7 Aug 2026 05.46 EDT Share Prefer the Guardian on Google The England striker Ivan Toney has been charged with assault after an incident at a nightclub in Soho. The Metropolitan police said Ivan Toney, 30, of Northampton, was charged on 31 July with assault causing actual bodily harm. The force said the charge relates to an alleged assault that reportedly took place on 6 December last year at a nightclub on Wardour Street in central London. Toney, a striker for Saudi Pro League club Al-Ahli, will appear at Westminster magistrates court on 24 September. The Sun newspaper reported on 8 December that Toney had been arrested after an alleged head-butt.

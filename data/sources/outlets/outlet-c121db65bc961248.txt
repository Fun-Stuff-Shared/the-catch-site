Taking over TikTok is the viral “5 Things Worth the Money” trend, where creators are skipping the endless product roundups in favour of five tried-and-true favourites they actually repurchase, recommend and refuse to live without. It’s a refreshing shift that reflects how beauty lovers are shopping today: more intentionally. Instead of chasing every new launch, consumers are investing in products that consistently deliver—and increasingly, many of those favourites are coming from the drugstore. Few brands embody the trend better than Maybelline New York , whose affordable, high-performance formulas continue to earn cult status alongside prestige beauty buys. If we had to narrow it down, these five deserve a spot on the list: Instant Age Rewind Concealer: The iconic concealer beloved for brightening, concealing and smoothing the under-eye area. Lifter Gloss: A glossy lip essential that delivers shine, hydration and all-day comfort. Cloudtopia: A lightweight lip and cheek colour with an airy texture and soft-blur finish. Lifter Liner: A creamy, long-wearing lip liner designed to define, shape and pair perfectly with the Lifter Gloss collection. Lifter Stix: An easy-to-use glow stick that adds effortless radiance wherever you need it. The takeaway? You don’t have to splurge to find products you’ll use down to the very last swipe.

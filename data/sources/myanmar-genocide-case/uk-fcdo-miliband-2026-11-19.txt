Foreign Secretary statement on International Court of Justice Myanmar judgment: 19 November - GOV.UK

Statement from the Foreign Secretary on International Court of Justice Myanmar judgment
Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband said:
“Today, the International Court of Justice has delivered a landmark judgment, finding that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.
The UK reaffirms our full support for the Court and the international legal order, and condemns the horrific treatment of the Rohingya people by the Myanmar military regime.
The UK is determined to act and ensure accountability for these appalling crimes.
We are taking immediate action today in light of this judgment. Building on existing sanctions against the Myanmar regime, we are ordering the expulsion of the Myanmar Defence Attaché from the UK and calling urgently for a UN Security Council session to discuss the judgment.
We must ensure the Court’s ruling sends a clear message that such crimes cannot be committed with impunity.
The Rohingya have endured years of appalling violence, displacement and persecution. Myanmar must comply fully with the judgment. The international community must now play its part in supporting implementation, accountability and justice for the Rohingya.
We are also summoning Myanmar’s Chargé d’Affaires, and will set out further action in the coming days to ensure those responsible are held to account. We remain steadfast in our support for the Rohingya and for a peaceful, democratic and inclusive future for Myanmar.”

SENATE BILL           660
AN ACT           relative to photo identification cards issued solely for the purpose of voting.

SPONSORS:        Sen. Gray, Dist 6

COMMITTEE:       Election Law and Municipal Affairs

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                                              ANALYSIS

   This bill amends where a person may obtain a photo identification card voucher form to exempt
them from the voter identification card fee.

                                         STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE

                                In the Year of Our Lord Two Thousand Twenty-Six

     AN ACT             relative to photo identification cards issued solely for the purpose of voting.

              Be it Enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court convened:


1        1 Administration of Motor Vehicle Laws; Powers and Duties; Identification Cards. Amend RSA
2    260:21, V to read as follows:
3             V.(a) The fee for such card shall be $20 and is not refundable, except that no fee shall be
4    charged to any person who, for reason of health or age, turns in his or her driver's license before the
5    expiration date of such license. For purposes of this section, reasons of age shall be deemed to apply
6    only to those persons over age 65. A person who requires a photo identification card only for voter
7    identification purposes may obtain a voucher in the form provided for in subparagraph (b) from his
8    or her town or city clerk [or the secretary of state] exempting the voter from the identification card
9    fee. Upon presentation of the voucher to the division, the actual costs of issuing the card shall be
10   paid by the secretary of state from the election fund established under RSA 5:6-d. An identification
11   card paid for by the secretary of state shall be valid for voter identification purposes only, and the
12   card, which shall be known as a voter identification card, shall be marked "for voter identification
13   only."
14               (b) The identification card voucher shall be in the following form:
15   IDENTIFICATION CARD VOUCHER
16   To be completed by applicant:
17   Name: ____________________
18   Domicile Address: ____________________
19   Mailing Address: ____________________
20   I hereby swear or affirm that I require a photo identification for voting purposes and that I do not
21   possess a form of identification that meets the requirements of the election laws of this state.
22   I hereby swear or affirm that I am the identical person whom I represent myself to be and that to the
23   best of my knowledge and belief the information above is true and correct.
24   ________________________
25   (Signature of applicant)
26   To be completed by town or city clerk:
27   I, the clerk of __________ (town or city), hereby swear or affirm that the person identified on this
28   voucher [is listed on the voter checklist] presented proof of domicile in this city or town.
29   _________________________________
30   (Signature of town or city clerk)

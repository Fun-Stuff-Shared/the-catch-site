                           STUDENT DOMICILE

 A student may lawfully claim domicile in the NH municipality where
 they reside while attending school, rather than where they reside when
 not attending school.



 The place you claim as your domicile must be the one place, more than
 any other place, where you have established a physical presence and
 intend to make that place your residence.

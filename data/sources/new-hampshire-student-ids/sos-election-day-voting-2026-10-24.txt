Election Day Voting
Information on election day requirements as a registered voter.
[NOTICE: On October 22, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court stayed a decision by the district court related to the use of student identification cards. This guidance has been updated to accurately reflect the Supreme Court's decision]
All registered voters in New Hampshire are required to show proof of their identification at their polling location to receive a ballot and vote.
Registered voters do not need to bring proof of age, domicile, or U.S. citizenship with them to the polls to vote. This proof is only required when registering to vote in New Hampshire. For more information on registering to vote, visit our Registering to Vote page.
What type of identification (ID) does a voter need to vote?
By statute, only the following forms of identification may expressly be used to prove identity.
- Driver’s license issued by any state or federal government;
- Non-driver ID card issued by NH DMV or motor vehicle agency of another state;
- Photo ID card for “voting identification only” issued by NH DMV (RSA 260:21);
- United States armed services identification card; or
- United States passport or passcard.
An acceptable photo ID must have an expiration date or date of issuance. The ID will remain valid, for voting purposes, for 5 years beyond the expiration date. If the voter is 65 or older, an acceptable photo ID may be used without regard to expiration date. The name on the ID shall substantially conform to the name on the checklist.
If a voter does not have one of the qualifying IDs listed above, they will be directed to the supervisors of the checklist to see if the voter’s identity can be verified. The supervisors of the checklist will determine if a moderator, clerk, supervisor, or selectman can verify the voter’s identity through personal knowledge.
Free Photo Identification Card for Voting Purposes Only
If you do not have an approved photo ID you may obtain photo identification for voting purposes only, free of charge, from the NH DMV. Obtain a voucher from your local town or city clerk or the Secretary of State to present to the DMV. Information on the documents you will need to present to the DMV is available here, see "Proof of Identity and Residency": www.dmv.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt416/files/inline-documents/dsmv634b.pdf

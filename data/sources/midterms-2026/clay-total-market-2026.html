Method

  • Universe: every security listed on NYSE, Nasdaq or NYSE American with a reported market capitalization, one line per company (the share class with the highest trading volume). See the Clay Total Market Index and its methodology.
  • Market capitalization: company-level, as reported by the data vendor at the close. Not float-adjusted.
  • Total: sum of company market capitalizations. Published indices that apply float adjustment and a US-company test report lower totals; see S&P TMI and Morningstar US Total Market.
  • Dates: each archive file is dated the day after the close it holds. Dates on this page are close dates.
  • Data: latest JSON; dated JSON and CSV files at /data/clay-total-market-index-YYYY-MM-DD.json and .csv.

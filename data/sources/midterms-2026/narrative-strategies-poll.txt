NARRATIVE STRATEGIES/535 NEWS MIDTERM VOTER POLL

METHODOLOGY

      The survey was conducted by Narrative Strategies online among a national sample of voter file-verified midterm voters using non-
      probability sampling. Respondents were recruited through an online sample exchange using demographic quotas to ensure
      balance across key groups based on public estimates of the likely electorate. Quotas were set for gender, age, race/ethnicity,
      region, urban/rural residence, and education to approximate the composition of the likely electorate. Measures to ensure data
      quality included steps to prevent duplicate responses, screening questions designed to identify inattentive respondents, and the
      removal of respondents who completed a substantial portion of the survey significantly faster than typical.

      The final sample was weighted to the profile of the 2026 midterm electorate, as measured by the Voter Poll by SSRS. Weights
      adjust for 2026 U.S. House vote, age, gender, race and ethnicity, education, party identification, ideology, union household, and
      household income. DEM reflects Democrats and Democratic-leaning independents, and GOP reflects Republicans and
      Republican-leaning independents. All respondents were matched to the voter file. Percentages may not sum to 100% because of
      rounding or because respondents could select more than one answer.

      Because this survey uses a non-probability sample, a traditional margin of sampling error does not strictly apply. A comparable
      estimate, adjusted for weighting, is ±3.0 percentage points for the national sample. This survey was fielded among N=1,006
      registered voters nationwide, including 499 Democrats and Democratic-leaning independents and 427 Republicans and
      Republican-leaning independents. The survey was fielded from October 24 - November 3, 2026.

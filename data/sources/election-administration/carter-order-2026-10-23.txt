RESTRAINING ORDER
The Court has just received another flurry of filings by the Parties, including the
United States’ Ex Parte Motion for an Injunction Pending Appeal (Dkt. No. 76) and State
Defendants’ Opposition to Plaintiff’s Motion (Dkt No. 79).
On October 19, 2026, the United States filed an Ex Parte Application for a
Temporary Restraining Order requesting a ruling “by November 3, 2026, before the end
of voting in the 2026 federal election.” Dkt. No. 22-2 at 4.
This case was transferred to this Court on Wednesday, October 21, 2026. Dkt. No.
46. Later that day, the United States formally filed its Application for a Temporary
Restraining Order, again requesting a ruling “by November 3, 2026.” Dkt. No. 62-1 at 4.
Pursuant to the United States’ Application, the Court set oral argument on the TRO for
Tuesday, October 27, 2026, one week before the United States’ requested date of relief—
November 3, 2026. Dkt. No. 64. After the Court set the hearing date, later the same
evening of October 21, the United States requested a ruling before noon the following
day, October 22, 2026. Dkt. No. 67.

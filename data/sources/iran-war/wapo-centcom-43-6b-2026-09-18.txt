Iran war cost is now at least $43.6 billion, U.S. Central Command says - The Washington Post

U.S. Central Command, which oversees American military operations in the Middle East, estimates that the Iran war has cost about $43.6 billion through early September, according to a document reviewed by The Washington Post.

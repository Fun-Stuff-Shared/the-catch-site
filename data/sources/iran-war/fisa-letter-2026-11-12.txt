Dear Speaker Johnson, Leader Thune, Leader Schumer, and Leader Jeffries:

On behalf ofthe President, we urge you to reauthorize Title VII of the Foreign Intelligence
Surveillance Act, including Section 702, no later than December 9, 2026. Any delays beyond this
date will risk this critical authority lapsing, putting the lives of bothAmericans in the homeland
and our troops stationed abroad at risk in the heightened threat environment briefed to the Gang
of 8 earlier this week.


As you know, Section 702 is the single most indispensable tool we have to keep our homeland
and the American people safe. It enables our Intelligence Community to detect and disrupt
terrorist plots, cyber intrusions, and weapons proliferation. No other authority can replace it.
Because the underlying statutory authority lapsed earlier this year, collection continues only
under existing certifications that will expire March 16, 2027. To prevent a gap in critical
intelligence, the government must start the 2027 renewal process no later than December 9,
2026.


The December 9 date stems from the statutory and judicial review process:

   •    Court review. While the rules ofthe Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC)
        permit a seven-day read copy filing, long-standing precedent requires the Department of
        Justice to submit draft certifications at least 30 days in advance.
        Filing. The signed certifications must be filed by January 8, 2027, 30 days after
        December 9 .
        Amicus review. Under the Reforming Intelligence and SecuringAmerica Act, the Court
        generally appoints amici, which extends its review to 60 days or more.
        FISC order. Reauthorization by December 9 is necessary for the FISC to issue an order
        authorizing Section 702 collection by March 9, 2027- a week before current collection
        ends .

Operation Epic Fury (OEF)
This report describes the activities of the U.S. Government in support of OEF as well as the work of the Department of War, the Department of State, and other U.S. Government agencies to further the U.S. Government’s policy goals with respect to Iran. It also discusses the planned, ongoing, and completed oversight work conducted by the Lead IG agencies and our partner oversight agencies. This report covers the period through June 30, 2026.

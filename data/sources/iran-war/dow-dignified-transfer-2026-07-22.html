Nation's Leaders Honor the Fallen During Dignified Transfer

July 22, 2026 | By David Vergun, Pentagon News |

President Donald J. Trump; Secretary of War Pete Hegseth; Air Force Gen. Dan Caine, chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff; and others attended a dignified transfer today to honor four soldiers killed in the U.S. Central Command region.

The deaths are related to Iranian aggression.

Army 1st Lt. Tyler James Feehan, 25, of Ewa Beach, Hawaii, was killed July 18, 2026, from injuries suffered during an enemy attack on July 17, 2026, at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base, Jordan.

About the Dignified Transfer

16:25

A dignified transfer is the process by which, upon the return from the theater of operations to the United States, the fallen are transferred from the aircraft at Dover Air Force Base, Delaware, to an awaiting vehicle that transfers the fallen service member to Air Force Mortuary Affairs Operations at Dover.

The dignified transfer is not a ceremony. Rather, it is a solemn movement of the transfer case by a carry team composed of military personnel from the fallen member's service. A senior ranking officer of the fallen member's service presides over each dignified transfer.

It is the War Department's policy to return America's fallen to their loved ones as quickly as possible.

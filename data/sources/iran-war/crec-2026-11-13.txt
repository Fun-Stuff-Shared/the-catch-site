PRAYER
The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret
Grun Kibben, offered the following
prayer:
O God, our provider, let this be the
day that we seek You. May we cease
our own pursuits and pursue the righteousness that You desire from us.
Today, may we show our faithfulness
to You in the way we love one another:
family members, friends, strangers,
and even our enemies. May we strive to
demonstrate that love which You have
so mercifully shown to us.
Then, we ask for the reward of a life
lived in You, where our hunger for decency and civility is satisfied, where
our thirst for integrity and honesty is
quenched.
Bless us this day and reveal to us
that all that we seek and everything
we desire is found in You.
In Your sure and certain name, we
pray.

the public service of the First African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church of San Jose, CA,
as it celebrates 162 years of service to our
community.
The African Methodist Episcopal Zion
Church, the Mother Church, was founded in
New York City in October 1796 and came to
be known as the ‘‘Freedom Church’’ for its unwavering commitment to the dignity and the
emancipation of Black Americans. Its legacy of
justice, empowerment, and faith has shaped
countless lives across generations.
Founded in San Jose in 1864, the First
American Methodist Episcopal Zion Church
has carried forward this profound legacy. Recognized as the oldest Black church in San
Jose, it has stood as a historic institution devoted to advancing equity, nurturing community, and strengthening the moral and spiritual
fabric of the city.
For more than a century and a half, the First
African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church has
provided service, guidance, fellowship, stewardship, educational support, economic assistance, and spiritual encouragement to the people of San Jose. The church has served as a
welcoming and steadfast pillar of community
life, opening its doors to all.
On November 15, 2026, the church will celebrate its 162nd anniversary. This milestone
commemorates not only its enduring presence
but also its ongoing efforts to strengthen, support, and uplift the San Jose community.
The First African Methodist Episcopal Zion
Church has stood for devotion, perseverance,
and civic leadership. Through its outreach and
its enduring dedication to justice and service,
the church has enriched countless lives. Its
longstanding contributions will continue to be
honored with deep gratitude and appreciation
throughout the San Jose community.

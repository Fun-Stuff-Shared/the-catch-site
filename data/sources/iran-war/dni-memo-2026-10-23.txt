‘TO: The Honorable Marco Rubio, Secretary ofState and National Security Adviser
FROM: The Honorable Jay Clayton, Director of National Intelligence
RE: Downgrading of Intelligence Information and Analysis with Regard to Lethal Plotting
Against Americans in the Middle East and Certain Other Countries
DATE: October 23, 2026
Secretary Rubio:
Recognizing that the safety of U.S. government personnel abroad is a top priority for the State
Department, the Intelligence Community, and the Trump Administration as a whole, it has
become clear that certain intelligence and analysis regarding Iranian efforts to target U.S.
government officials in the Middle East and certain other areas are relevant to more individuals
than are currently cleared to receive this information.
‘Specifically, through various channels, we have received intelligence that Iran has substantially
increased efforts to target U.S. personnel. The lethal plotting efforts by the Iranians against U.S.
citizens have reached advanced stages in both the Middle East and certain other countries.
‘These materials have been provided to appropriately-cleared senior staff    on your team and we
are available to brief you at your convenience. We will make every effort to downgrade as much
intelligence as possible so you can share it with State Department personnel at your discretion.

ce: The Honorable John Ratcliffe, Director of the Central Intelligence Agency

      The Kingdom of Bahrain condemns the continued brutal Iranian attacks on its territory and holds Iran fully responsible for the consequences.

      Sunday 5 Jumada II 1448AH 15-11-2026AD
      one minute

      Manama (UNA/BNA) – The Bahraini Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed the Kingdom of Bahrain’s strongest condemnation of the continued Iranian aggression against its territory, including the attack launched today, Sunday, November 15, 2026, by drones that were intercepted and destroyed by air defense systems in the Kingdom’s airspace. This comes two days after a similar attack at dawn on Friday, November 13, 2026, by two drones that were also intercepted. The Iranian Revolutionary Guard explicitly claimed responsibility for these attacks. The Ministry praised the efficiency, vigilance, and capability of the Bahrain Defense Force in confronting today’s attack and its complete readiness to deter any aggression.

      The Ministry considered these attacks a resumption of Iran’s sinful attacks on the Kingdom and the countries of the region, disregarding the provisions of international law and the principles of the Charter of the United Nations. The Kingdom considers them a blatant aggression against its sovereignty and territorial integrity, a flagrant violation of Security Council Resolution No. 2817, which condemned the Iranian attacks on the Kingdom of Bahrain and neighboring countries and demanded that they be stopped immediately, a targeting of safe citizens and residents, a sabotage of de-escalation efforts, and a serious threat to the security and stability of the region.

      The Ministry affirmed the Kingdom of Bahrain’s categorical rejection of the flimsy pretexts and false claims put forward by the Iranian Revolutionary Guard, stressing that no pretext justifies aggression against the sovereignty of states and the integrity of their territories, and that freedom of navigation and passage, by sea and air, in the straits used for international navigation is a right guaranteed by the rules of international law, and is not subject to the will or conditions of any state. It also rejects turning its territory into an arena for settling scores, and holds Iran fully responsible for these attacks and their consequences.

      The Ministry also affirmed that the Kingdom of Bahrain retains its inherent right, both individually and collectively, to self-defense in accordance with Article (51) of the Charter, and to take all necessary measures to preserve its sovereignty and the security of its citizens and residents on its land, in close coordination with its brothers in the Cooperation Council for the Arab States of the Gulf, based on the Joint Defense Agreement, which considers an attack on any member state as an attack on all of them, and with its allies and partners.

      The Ministry noted that the Kingdom of Bahrain, as a member of the UN Security Council, calls on the Council to condemn these repeated attacks, and demands that it assume its responsibilities and take decisive measures to ensure Iran’s full compliance with Resolution 2817, and to stop its attacks, threats and support for proxy groups in the region.

      The Ministry of Foreign Affairs added that the Kingdom of Bahrain affirms that its security is a red line, that its sovereignty is non-negotiable, and that it will remain, as it has always been, an advocate for good neighborliness, respect for the sovereignty of states and non-interference in their affairs, and keen on the security and stability of the Arabian Gulf.

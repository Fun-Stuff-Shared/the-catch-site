Important Notice

Two of MIA’s four runways remain closed. Cancellations continue due to aircraft and flight crews not having arrived. Passengers with flights scheduled today should check directly with their airline for flight updates before heading to the airport.

 

Local Transit Alert: Travelers using Metrorail will need to transfer at Earlington Heights station for the Orange Line train to MIA.

Department of State FY 2027 Budget Request: A Commitment to America First Foreign Policy | Jun 3, 2026 | Hearing Highlights

Summary
- Marco Rubio (Secretary of State, U.S. Department of State) said Operation Epic Fury destroyed Iran's navy, air force and 80-90% of its defense industrial base.
- Rubio said Venezuelan oil proceeds sit in a Citibank blocked account audited by KPMG and spent only on jointly approved expenditures.
- Rep. Dean (D, PA-4) pressed Rubio on Palestinian deaths at Gaza Humanitarian Foundation sites and Rubio blamed Hamas for hijacking aid.
- Democrats condemned the Iran war's costs and corruption concerns while Republicans praised decisive action preventing an Iranian nuclear weapon development.
- Rubio pledged to comply with ANARA on any Iran agreement and to provide a classified briefing on blocking funds to Hezbollah and Hamas.

HomeDepartment Reports and Publications …Report on Venezuela Custody Account Operations hide Report on Venezuela Custody Account Operations Report This report provides a comprehensive account of the Venezuelan natural resource revenues deposited in, held by, and disbursed from the U.S. Treasury Venezuela Custody Account (also referred to as the Venezuela Custody Account), a deposit fund account established consistent with Executive Order (E.O.) 14373, “Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People.” It documents the financial oversight, governance, and control mechanisms implemented by the U.S. Department of State and its contracted monitor and advisor, KPMG, to promote transparency and accountability in the management of these funds.

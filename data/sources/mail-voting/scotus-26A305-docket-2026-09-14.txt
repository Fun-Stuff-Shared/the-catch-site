 Application (26A305) referred to the Court. 

 
 
 
 
 

 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Sep 14 2026

 Application (26A305) for stay presented to Justice Jackson and by her referred to the Court is denied. The Government is unlikely to succeed on the merits of its challenge to the District Court's preliminary injunction. And the equitable factors applicable for obtaining emergency relief from this Court do not favor a stay. Justice Kavanaugh, concurring. (Detached Opinion ) Justice Alito, joined by Justice Thomas, dissenting. (Detached Opinion )

       Re:     United States Postal Service v. California, No. 26A297

Dear Mr. Harris:

         On Thursday, September 3, 2026, the federal government filed in the above-captioned
case an application for a stay of an order issued on August 27, 2026, by the United States District
Court for the District of Massachusetts. Labeled as a 14-day temporary restraining order, but
effectively functioning as a preliminary injunction, that order barred the implementation or
enforcement of several sections of a Postal Service rule regarding the mailing of federal-election
ballots.

        On Friday, September 4, 2026, the district court formally issued a preliminary injunction
that was substantively identical to its August 27 order, which it purported to dissolve. The
United States appealed that injunction the same evening, and it filed a motion the next day in the
United States Court of Appeals for the First Circuit to stay the new order pending appeal. Earlier
today, in an abundance of caution, the United States filed in this Court a new application for a
stay of the renewed preliminary injunction. In light of those developments, and to simplify
proceedings, the United States withdraws its September 3 stay application in the above-captioned
case.


                                             Sincerely,



                                             D. John Sauer

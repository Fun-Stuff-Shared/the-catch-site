Supreme Court allows New Hampshire to fully ban student IDs for voting in midterms

The Supreme Court has cleared the way for New Hampshire to fully ban student IDs for voting during the midterm elections

1:26
The Supreme Court is pictured Monday, Oct. 5, 2026, in Washington. (AP Photo/Mariam Zuhaib)
The Associated Press
ByLINDSAY WHITEHURST Associated Press
October 22, 2026, 7:13 PM

WASHINGTON -- The Supreme Court cleared the way Thursday for New Hampshire to fully ban the use of student IDs for voting during the midterm elections.

The justices granted a Republican-backed emergency appeal and blocked the use of student IDs as a backup method of identification. The Supreme Court cited a longstanding legal principle that judges shouldn’t change voting rules too close to Election Day.

Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson was the only justice to publicly dissent.

New Hampshire passed a law this year that said student IDs would no longer be an expressly accepted form of identification for obtaining a ballot. The secretary of state then issued guidance barring them as a backup way to prove a voter's identity.

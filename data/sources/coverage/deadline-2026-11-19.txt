Trump Defamation Suit Against New York Times Moves Forward As Dismissal Fails

Donald Trump‘s never ending war against what the former Apprentice host loves to term “fake news” just scored a win against the New York Times in the Florida courts. A win that could come with a $15 billion price tag down the legal line.
Which means, the Gray Lady and Penguin Random House must be feeling, as Grand Theft Auto VI’s Lucia says in the just released blockbuster video game: “Bad luck, I guess.”
Gratuitous but satisfying GTA VI reference aside, Tampa-based US District Judge Steven Merryday’s order Thursday takes some sharp digs at the NYT, and the publisher over the 2024 book Lucky Loser: How Donald Trump Squandered His Father’s Fortune and Created The Illusion of Success and a subsequent piece in the paper. Trump sued all concerned for $15 billion back in September last year, claiming “the newspaper’s editorial routine is now one of industrial-scale defamation and libel against political opponents” – a.k.a. him. That suit was tossed out, but Trump was allowed to refile and then in July the defendants filed their motion to dismiss.
While stripping journalists and Lucky Loser authors Russ Buettner and Susanne Craig from the big bucks action, as well as the NYT’s chief White House correspondent Peter Baker, Judge Merryday pretty brazenly did hold PRH and the paper’s feet to the qualified fire over published unsubstantiated statements that Trump committed the series of tax crimes over the years.

In refusing the Times and the publisher’s motion to dismiss the case, the George W. Bush appointed judge wrote: “Anyone who publishes the claim that someone else has committed a crime of the complex nature described in some of the challenged statements and who has not received an explicit opinion from a qualified source after a thorough examination of pertinent facts necessarily proceeds with particularized reason to doubt the truth of their statements and with knowledge of a high degree of probable falsity of the statements; in other words, they know that they don’t know, but they are undeterred. Knowing of the necessity for that opinion but failing to procure that opinion is a fine example of the purposeful avoidance of the truth, a knowing failure that remains actionable.”
With the case now set to move towards further discovery and trial, it may or may not be resolved before Trump is supposed to leave the White House in 2029. What is certain is that fresh off his very reality TV stage managed meeting with Russia’s Putin and China’s Xi, Trump will be crowing about this win for a while.
As his team did today.
“President Trump continues holding the Fake News accountable for deceiving the American people, a spokesperson for POTUS’ lawyers told Deadline after the order appeared in the court docket.
“Judge Merryday has correctly denied the motion to dismiss filed by The New York Times, Penguin Publishing, and their writers, as to President Trump’s powerhouse defamation lawsuit,” the flack continued. “These Defendants will now have to answer for intentionally and maliciously defaming President Trump. The New York Times has a long pattern of defaming President Trump in service of its leftist political agenda, and today’s momentous ruling is a victory for honesty in America.”
On the flip side, having enduring a plethora of Trump attacks and suits over the years, the Gray Lady hasn’t given up the fight.
“The Times is encouraged to see Judge Merryday’s dismissal of almost all claims against us,’ the NYT’s Charlie Stadtlander stated today. “We continue to believe the remaining claim in the filing has no legal merit. We look forward to making this argument as we continue to defend ourselves against this baseless attack on The Times and the rights of the free and independent press in this country.”

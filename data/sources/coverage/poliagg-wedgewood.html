We've removed every poll from Wedgewood Polls

On October 20, 2026, Wedgewood Polls publicly admitted it had fabricated the data behind its polls, after the polling-news site FiftyPlusOne questioned its credentials and data. The New York Times, Decision Desk HQ and other aggregators have also removed its polls.

We removed all 43 of its polls, across 19 races, on October 22, 2026. They no longer count toward any polling average, forecast, chart or poll table on this site, and any new release from this firm is excluded automatically. They are listed below only so you can see what was removed.

Commerce Department Moves to Crack Down on Chip Exports - WSJ

WASHINGTON—The Commerce Department issued a long-awaited rule to crack down on shipments of chips from Nvidia and other U.S. companies to China, the latest sign of how the Jensen Huang saga is influencing the Trump administration’s policies.
The new proposed rule would add Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore and Brazil to a list of countries subject to export restrictions. Companies would need licenses from the Commerce Department to ship to those countries, a step intended to prevent Chinese customers from accessing data-center capacity in those countries remotely. U.S. tech companies could get exceptions.

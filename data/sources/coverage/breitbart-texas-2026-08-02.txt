Texas Leftist Talarico Gains Two-Point Edge over Paxton in Senate Race

Editor’s Note: The firm cited in this story, Wedgewood Polling, has now admitted it was faking its data after an investigator reached out to the company with evidence of fraudulent polls. In a statement, Wedgewood said its operation was “entirely fabricated” as a social “experiment” to expose data aggregators and “mainstream political narratives,” which affect election outcomes.

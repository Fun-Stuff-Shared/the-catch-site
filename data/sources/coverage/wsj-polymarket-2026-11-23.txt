Two Groups of Polymarket Accounts Made Over $340,000 on Huang Comeback
Two “suspicious clusters” of Polymarket accounts collectively made more than $340,000 by correctly betting that Jensen Huang would return to Nvidia, according to Bubblemaps, a blockchain analytics firm.
All of the accounts bet exclusively on Nvidia-related markets and began placing bets within minutes of when their creators deposited funds in them, according to Bubblemaps. The first group of five accounts was funded Nov. 16, while the second group of 11 accounts was funded Monday, just hours before Nvidia’s board announced it was reinstating Huang as CEO, Bubblemaps said.
One of the accounts, which doesn’t have a username, started placing bets soon after 2 a.m. ET, Polymarket data shows. The anonymous account bought contracts that would pay out if Huang rejoined Nvidia by the end of November, which only cost 3 cents apiece—meaning that the market saw only a 3% chance of such a scenario happening—as well as a similar contract that would pay out if Huang returned by the end of December.

Supposed Ken Paxton call to investigate Senate defeat is fabricated | Fact Check

- Published on November 4, 2026 at 22:49
- 2 min read
- By Gwen Roley, AFP USA
After Texas elected a Democrat to the US Senate for the first time in decades, some social media users are claiming defeated Republican Ken Paxton called for an investigation into James Talarico's victory. But the apparent statement is fabricated; Paxton conceded the race, and the supposed NBC News NOW graphic cited as evidence never appeared during the network's election night coverage.
"It is simply not possible for somebody with the radical ideology of James Talarico to win an election in Texas fairly," reads the statement attributed to Paxton in an image shared November 4, 2026 on X.
"I call upon the Senators and Representatives of this country, as well as our President, to take whatever action necessary to secure our elections, investigate thoroughly and ensure that the results of these elections are fair," the alleged quote continues.
Similar posts across X, Facebook, Bluesky and Reddit spread the same image, an apparent screenshot lifted from a broadcast by the American TV network NBC News.
US President Donald Trump is faced with a transformed political landscape after voters in the November 3 midterm elections handed control of the Congress to the Democrats in a stunning rebuke of the Republican leader's presidency.
Paxton, Texas's attorney general and a close Trump ally, lost to Talarico, a former schoolteacher and Presbyterian seminarian, dealing a major blow to Republicans in the historically conservative southern border state (archived here).
The closely watched US Senate race sparked several false claims that AFP debunked.
The purported quote calling for an investigation into the election attributed to Paxton on social media is similarly fabricated.
Keyword searches did not reveal any legitimate reporting about Paxton having released such a statement.
Paxton did make a brief concession speech before midnight in Texas on November 3, congratulating Talarico on his victory (archived here).
The nonpartisan Texas Association of County Election officials released a statement on November 4 commending the state's delivery of a "fair, accurate, and secure" election.
Furthermore, a search of the transcript generated from NBC's live election night broadcast -- available on Facebook and other streaming services -- found no mention of such a quote from Paxton (archived here).
The graphic used as evidence includes a chyron that does not match the "Decision 2026" design NBC News used to animate its reporting on election results.
AFP reached out to NBC News and the Paxton campaign about the falsely attributed remarks, but responses were not immediately forthcoming.

It’s Election Day. Here’s what to watch as Texas wraps its most competitive electoral season in years.
The Texas Tribune, published 2026-11-03
Excerpt returned by the Parallel extract service; capture and Wayback refused the page.

Although turnout is high across the board, it is most pronounced in counties that went blue in the last two presidential elections. The early vote turnout in blue and swing counties is 89% of what it was in 2024 and 135% of what it was in the 2022 midterms. In red counties, those numbers drop to 77% compared to 2024 and 125% compared to 2022.

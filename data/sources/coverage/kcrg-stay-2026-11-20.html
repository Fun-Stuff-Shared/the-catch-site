BREAKING: Appeals court pauses lead azide detonation

Appeals court pauses lead azide detonation

By KCRG Staff
Published: Nov. 20, 2026 at 11:36 AM CST|Updated: 8 minutes ago
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

DES MOINES, Iowa (KCRG) - The U.S. Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals has granted an emergency injunction to pause the lead azide detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa.

Court documents do not say how long the pause will be.

The Iowa Environmental Council and the Iowa chapter of the Sierra Club filed the lawsuit on Thursday.

The explosion, which was delayed to Monday, would detonate nearly five tons of bomb-making chemicals.

The lawsuit challenges an emergency permit the Environmental Protection Agency granted to the U.S. Army for the detonation.

The groups allege in their lawsuit that many Iowans did not receive information about potential lead exposure.

“Our federal government needs to do better at communicating the potential health risks of lead contamination in our communities and providing more time to keep our communities and vulnerable groups safe, like children and pregnant women,” said Sarah Green, the Executive Director at the Iowa Environmental Council.

The groups also criticized the Army’s decision to proceed with the detonation.

“Iowans deserve to see an Environmental and Health Impact Statement and investigation into better alternatives than this extreme planned explosion. We’re hoping the Judge halts this action before it takes place tomorrow,” said Wally Taylor, Attorney and Legal Chair for the Sierra Club Iowa Chapter.

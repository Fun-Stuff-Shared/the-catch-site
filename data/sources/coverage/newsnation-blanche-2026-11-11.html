Todd Blanche among list of administration officials living on military bases

Libbey Dean

Updated: Nov 12, 2026 / 05:40 AM CST
Not sure how to find NewsNation on your TV? Find your channel here.  Get 24/7 fact-based unbiased news coverage with the NewsNation app.

 

(NewsNation) — Attorney General Todd Blanche has been living on a U.S. military base since early 2026, according to multiple sources.

Blanche is part of a growing number of senior Trump administration officials who have moved into military housing, many amid security concerns.

Scott Bessent moves onto military base after Iranian threats, NYT reports

That list includes Secretary of State Marco Rubio, War Secretary Pete Hegseth, White House chief of staff Stephen Miller and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent.

Bessent was recently relocated to a military base over concerns for his safety, including threats from Iran, according to sources.

Since August, Bessent has led the economic squeeze on Tehran, with tougher sanctions and tighter limits on trade routes. The goal is to cut off cash to the Revolutionary Guard, the military arm that also controls much of Iran’s economy. 

House subcommittee talks ‘serious risks’ of sports betting on campuses

Blanche is protected by the FBI, which provides executive protection to the attorney general.

Blanche’s predecessor, former Attorney General Pam Bondi, was also moved to heavily guarded quarters at a military installation in the Washington, D.C., area after receiving threats.

The FBI declined to comment to NewsNation, and NewsNation has also reached out to the Justice Department for comment.

Is the 2026 Mississippi Senate race competitive?

What does the latest poll of the 2026 Mississippi Senate race say?

How is the PollingSource average calculated?

The PollingSource average is the unweighted mean of the last three scientific public polls, or fewer when fewer exist. One poll sets the rating when it ended in the last 30 days.

U.S. Marine stationed in Okinawa arrested on suspicion of killing Japanese woman - UPI.com

Oct. 4 (UPI) -- Police in Okinawa arrested a U.S. Marine accused of robbing and killing a local Japanese woman, officials said.
Okinawa police arrested Devin Jacob Ballard, 20, an American service member stationed at U.S. Marine Corps Air Station Futenma, Japanese news outlet NHK reported.
He's accused of killing Yagi Anna, a 39-year-old woman between 2 a.m. and 4 a.m. Saturday at a hotel. Police said she died of suffocation from pressure on the neck.
Ballard also allegedly stole Anna's wallet and other belongings.
The III Marine Expeditionary Force said in a statement the branch was cooperating with Japanese authorities in its investigation.
"We extend our deepest condolences to the victim's family and friends," the statement said.
"The Marine Corps takes these allegations very seriously and expects every Marine to adhere to the highest standards of behavior. The Marine is in custody of local authorities while investigations proceed."
Security footage at the hotel showed a woman entering the hotel room along with another person who later left carrying items, NHK reported. The second person in the video allegedly matched the description of Ballard, including hair and tattoos.
Police arrested Ballard Saturday night in the entertainment district of the city. He denied the allegations.
Japanese officials lodged an official protest with the U.S. government in response to the incident.
Japanese Prime Minister Takaichi Sanae described the incident as extremely regrettable in a post on social media Sunday. She demanded full cooperation from U.S. authorities.

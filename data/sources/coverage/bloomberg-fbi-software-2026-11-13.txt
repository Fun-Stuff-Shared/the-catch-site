The Federal Bureau of Investigation doesn’t believe the communication issue affecting air traffic control was due to a cyberattack or criminality, said a person familiar with the matter. One area of inquiry is whether the outage was caused by a software update, the person said.
The Federal Aviation Administration posted Friday on its website that a ground stop affects all US airports and departing flights should remain grounded until further notice. It cited “extraordinary circumstances” and “reasons of safety.”

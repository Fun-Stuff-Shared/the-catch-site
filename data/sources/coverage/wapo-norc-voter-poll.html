How Americans voted in the midterms, by demographic group and in key states

A nationwide survey of voters by NORC examines how different demographic and political groups voted for Congress and in key Senate elections.

6 minutes ago
1 min
By Scott Clement
, 
John Muyskens
and 
Junne Joaquin Alcantara

See how different groups voted for the U.S. House and Senate in the 2026 midterm elections, according to preliminary results from the NORC Civic Health & Elections Project survey, which was sponsored by The Washington Post and other organizations. The survey interviewed more than 90,000 U.S. voters nationally in the week leading up to Election Day.

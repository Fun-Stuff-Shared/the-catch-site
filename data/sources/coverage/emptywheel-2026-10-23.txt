The Epistemology of Hunter Biden's "Laptop" - emptywheel

The first thing you should know about the complaint charging Nikki Floris and Brad Benavides with lying to Jim Jordan’s committee in 2023 is that they did not say the hard drive masquerading as a laptop that Rudy Giuliani rolled out in October 2020 was part of a Russian disinformation campaign.
They said they didn’t know.
On October 16, 2020, BENAVIDES wrote, and FLORIS approved, additions to the FBI Director’s talking points on the laptop for his call with Senator Mark Warner. The Office of Congressional Affairs’ draft stated: “The most I can say is that the FBI is in possession of an item that we lawfully seized.” BENAVIDES responded “This is good…you could add another if asked: Is the information we’re seeing in the press about a laptop containing emails associated with Hunter Biden part of a Russia disinformation effort? I can see from the perspective of anyone consuming this story that they could be concerned this is a Russia disinformation effort similar to what the FBI, ODNI, and other agencies have warned against; however, the FBI has not made that determination in this specific instance and as I stated before, I won’t comment on investigations or investigative actions.” He then wrote “Nikki, thoughts???” FLORIS responded “Looks good!”
So they can’t be the sources for any of a number of conspiracy theories that Jim Jordan, Kash Patel, and … everyone else claim to believe about 2020. They didn’t tell Politico, for example, that the Rudy Giuliani hard drive was Russian disinformation.
The second thing you should know is that the complaint substantiates materiality — an element of the charged offense — of what it claims are lies Floris and Benavides told House Judiciary Committee with what it euphemistically calls “information from a Congressional staffer.”
In connection with this investigation, the FBI has obtained information from a Congressional staffer who was present at the 2023 House Judiciary Committee interviews of BENAVIDES and FLORIS. The staffer confirmed that he or she viewed FLORIS’s statements concerning her involvement in and discussions of the Hunter Biden investigations as materially untrue. The staffer also confirmed that he or she viewed BENAVIDES’s statements on his awareness of and involvement in the Hunter Biden investigations as materially untrue. The staffer further stated that FLORIS’s and BENAVIDES’s answers influenced the Committee’s investigation significantly, including by thwarting investigative follow-up interviews and subpoenas. For example, had FLORIS answered the Committee’s questions concerning her awareness of discussions relating to Hunter Biden at the FITF truthfully, the Committee would have aggressively pursued all relevant information about those communications. Had BENAVIDES been truthful about his involvement in the Hunter Biden investigations, the Committee would have sought additional documents and testimony in furtherance of its investigation.
This “information” is horseshit. The investigation kept pumping out subpoenas and demands. More importantly, this pretends the goal was something other than killing the plea deal Hunter signed that summer, which the investigation achieved.
By treating this as “information” rather than “an interview,” the FBI may be attempting to avoid the necessity of jailing this Jordan staffer instead of Floris and Benavides. But I hope their attorneys insist on plopping that staffer on a stand sooner rather than later, because thus far, that staffer is making shit up to rationalize these charges, and doing so without any legal jeopardy himself.
Another critical detail to understand these charges: Everyone involved believes, as an article of faith, that the hard drive Rudy rolled out on October 2020, is the same thing as the laptop that John Paul Mac Isaac shared with the FBI in December 2019. Just as an example, after showing Benavides saying, on the day the NYPost story came out, “I would love to know if a copy [of the laptop] exists, and was it provided to anyone,” it then scolds that the next day, Benavides asks about the dissemination of the actual laptop.
The same day, BENAVIDES asked the Hunter Biden case team for specifics on the laptop that he already knew existed. BENAVIDES messaged FBI Employee 2 (one line is a separate conversation with another FBI employee redacted for clarity):
We are in the year of 2026, and we know the FBI did not, in 2020, forensically compare the Rudy Giuliani laptop with the one obtained directly from JPMI, and we also know that when introducing various devices into evidence at trial, the Cellebrite reports were substantially different. These are not hash matches. They are not the same device.
It’s not the same forensically. It did not have the same content. It is not the same technically.
And no one in 2020, or even in 2023, when Floris and Benavides allegedly lied, would have assumed the devices were the same! To do so would break every rule of forensic investigation taught at the FBI academy! It would be shoddy FBI work to do what this complaint demands these two FBI agents do.
But this is an article of faith. And like all true believers, one of the greatest law enforcement agencies in the world can no longer think of the Hunter Biden laptop and derivative hard drives as some hardware and some bytes that can be matched or not by forensic processes.
It is the laptop.
And so this criminal complaint treats “the laptop” as if the thing reported in the NY Post interview is as a matter of essence the same thing as the laptop JPMI had handed over a year earlier.
Which is to say, about half of the references to “the laptop” in this complaint are to the JPMI device (to make things worse, in one case the complaint claims that the FBI only got “the contents of Hunter Biden’s laptop,” which is false) and about half are references to the Rudy Giuliani hard drive, but the entire complaint treats them as if they are the same thing and — worse — as if Floris and Benavides would share what has become an object of faith 6 years later within hours of the first NYPost story.
Finally, you need to know that this complaint, rushed out 10 days before an election, is really poorly written. Perhaps most fatally, the complaint only belatedly explains the circumstances of the HJC testimony each gave at which they allegedly lied, and even then gives inconsistent explanations about the point of those hearings (which will undermine materiality claims). Which is how, 13 pages into the complaint, it introduces first the Chuck Grassley and Ron Johnson investigation (for which it relies on Benavides’ transcript) and then the defensive briefing about Andrii Telizhenko (for which it relies on the Floris transcript), and then having relied on both transcripts, introduces the interviews whence those transcripts were made.

E.g., Floris Tr. 28:15–19, 29:13–15.

Trump on Vance Blaming War for Losses: ‘Largely True’

By

Marianne LeVine

Photo: Evan Vucci/Reuters

President Trump said that Vice President JD Vance’s social-media post blaming Republican midterm election losses on the war in Iran was “largely true because it drove the gasoline up a little bit short term.”

But Trump added that he had no regrets about the war. “If I had to do it again, I’d have to do it again because long term, it’s the only answer,” Trump said. “You can’t let Iran have a nuclear weapon.”

Friday’s lead azide detonation delayed to Monday

IOWA (KCRG)- The controlled detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown has been delayed from Friday, Nov. 20, until Monday, Nov. 23, according to acting Army Secretary Adam Telle.
In a post on X, Telle said public safety is the Army’s top priority and led to the decision to delay the detonation.
Governor Kim Reynolds issued a statement saying, “Earlier today, I spoke with Acting Secretary of the Army Adam Telle and asked him to delay the planned detonation at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. I’m grateful for his decision to do so.”

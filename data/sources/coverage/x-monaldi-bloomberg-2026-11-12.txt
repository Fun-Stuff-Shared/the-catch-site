US-Held Venezuela Oil Sales Fund Paid Out $12.1 Billion
By Bloomberg News
(Bloomberg) -- Venezuela’s US-controlled custody account disbursed $12.1 billion between January and September, with the funds made up almost entirely of proceeds from crude oil, according to a US State Department report.
The account received about $12.3 billion from 20 approved depositors, while $168.6 million remained in custody as of Sept. 30 for future authorized disbursements, according to the report published Thursday. The US took custody of the revenue in January to shield Venezuelan oil proceeds from the country’s creditors.
Trafigura was the largest depositor, with $4.1b from trading of oil, gold and minerals over the period. Vitol deposited $3.44b from oil trading, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.

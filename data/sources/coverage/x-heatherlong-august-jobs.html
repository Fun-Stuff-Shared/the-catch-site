Heather Long on X: "August was a 'wow' jobs report. August jobs added: 162,000 (3x expectation of 55k) July: +21,000 (up from the original report of -23,000) June: +31,000 (up from 20,000) Unemployment rate: 4.1% (unchanged from July) Labor force participation rate: 61.6% (up from 61.4%) And it wasn't just healthcare hiring. Hospitality (+59,000) Education (+42,000) Construction (+22,000) Manufacturing (+16,000) So far in 2026, the economy is averaging +80,000 job gains a month. That's a big rebound from 10k/month in 2025."

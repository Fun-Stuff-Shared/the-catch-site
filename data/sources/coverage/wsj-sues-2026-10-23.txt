Trump Says He Directed Justice Department to Sue Alaska Over Senate Race - WSJ

WASHINGTON—President Trump said he asked the Justice Department to sue Alaska to prevent certification of the state’s Senate results if the Republican candidate loses, an extraordinary move by his administration to intervene legally in the 2026 midterm elections.
The Justice Department filed the lawsuit in federal court in Alaska, citing the potential for voter confusion because the ballot there includes retiree Dan J. Sullivan Jr. in addition to the Republican incumbent Sen. Dan S. Sullivan, who is in an extremely close race with Democratic challenger Mary Peltola.

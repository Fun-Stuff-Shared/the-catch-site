California attorney general says Huang reinstatement changes nothing

By Jody Godoy
Nov 23 (Reuters) - California Attorney General Rob Bonta's office said on Monday that Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia's chief executive officer does not diminish the need for a probe into what Nvidia told investors about its China ties.
Bonta started investigating the company last week after Huang's resigned amid revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman alleged to have spied for China. Nvidia reinstated Huang on Monday after US President Donald Trump called for his return.
"The reinstatement of Jensen Huang does not change the fact that the American people deserve to know what happened here — whether Nvidia misled investors and the public," a spokesperson for Bonta's office said on Monday
The office's probe into whether confidential information or advanced chips went to China, and whether the company misled investors, remains ongoing, the spokesperson added.
Nvidia said its board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Huang after concluding that it was satisfied with his candor and leadership ability. The board said that, after receiving input from senior US government officials, it saw no national security risk from his return.

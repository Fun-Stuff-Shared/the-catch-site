Well, not everyone treated Wedgewood as legit. RealClearPolitics, the pioneer poll aggregator, never included these bogus polls – either in the RCP Poll Average or in the voluminous number of surveys cited daily on our polling pages.

“RCP has a very high standard for including polls in our averages, which this pollster did not begin to meet,” explained Kavon W. Nikrad, RCP’s director of polling. “They were a new pollster with no track record to evaluate. They also had a ‘placeholder-style’ Google Sites website, had no staff directory on that website, and no social media history to look back on to establish that they were an actual, legitimate polling firm.”

“This is incredibly misleading,” tweeted Cooper Burton of FiftyPlusOne. His site, Burton added, “was planning to publish an investigation into Wedgewood's polls tomorrow morning, and reached out to them this morning for comment. This is just an attempt to save face after they realized we caught them.”

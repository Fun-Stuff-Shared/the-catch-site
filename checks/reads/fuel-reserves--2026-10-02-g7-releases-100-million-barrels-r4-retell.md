**1. Five sentences**
- On October 2 the G7 leaders agreed to release 100 million barrels of emergency oil over four months, with a lot of diesel up front.
- Those barrels were already promised in March, when governments pledged 400 million barrels after the Iran war began. About 100 million of that pledge was never released.
- The White House says Trump got Europe to hand over 100 million barrels of diesel. Germany says it's just the March obligation, with nothing new.
- Diesel hit a record in the U.S., and Washington threatened to ban diesel exports to Europe unless Europe released more. Trump then dropped the ban.
- An energy-agency board meets October 14–15 to decide who releases what.

**2. What it's really about**
A "new" 100-million-barrel deal that is mostly an old, unfinished promise, which Washington and Europe each describe in their own favor.

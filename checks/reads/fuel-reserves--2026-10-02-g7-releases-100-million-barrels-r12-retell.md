**1. Five sentences**
- In March the IEA's 32 member countries pledged 400 million barrels of emergency oil, and about 325 million had gone out by October 2.
- On October 2 the G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels over four months, diesel first. That was hours before Trump dropped his threat to ban U.S. diesel exports.
- The IEA says 100 million barrels is roughly what is still unreleased from March, so the G7 number may just be the old pledge finished. The White House calls it 100 million barrels of new European diesel.
- Germany says no additional quantities, Japan says no more, and France and Italy might add a little.
- The IEA board meets the week of October 12 to decide who actually releases what.

**2. What it's really about**
Whether the G7's "100 million barrels" is new oil or the March pledge relabeled.

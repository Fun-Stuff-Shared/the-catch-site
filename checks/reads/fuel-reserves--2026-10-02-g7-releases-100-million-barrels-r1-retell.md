**1. Five sentences**
- The G7 leaders agreed on October 2 to release 100 million barrels of emergency oil over four months, diesel first, through the International Energy Agency.
- The White House presents this as Europe giving the U.S. 100 million barrels of diesel after Trump pressed it.
- The G7 statement and Germany say it is the unfinished part of a 400-million-barrel pledge made in March, so almost nothing in it is new.
- Diesel hit a record $6.53 a gallon in September, so the U.S. pushed Europe, and Trump floated a diesel export ban.
- The agency's board meets October 14 and 15 to settle who releases what, while Hormuz traffic and a hurricane keep supply tight.

**2. What it's about**
A political "win" that is mostly an old promise being kept, and the two sides describe it differently.

**3. Where I got lost**
- "About 100 million of those barrels have not been released, the agency counts." Four hundred minus 325 is 75, not 100. The explanation ("Some countries released more than they promised, so what the rest still owe comes to about 100 million") only works after a re-read.
- "on September 29 it offered up to 40 million more." More than what? 133.5 plus 40 roughly equals 172, so it is the remainder, not extra. The catch section then says "about 38.5 million barrels of its own March share to award," which finally clarifies it.
- "Its exchanges with oil companies": "exchanges" is never explained.
- "Germany has released 600,000 tonnes of the 2.65 million tonnes": the unit switches from barrels with no conversion.
- "Crude found its way back to market. Fuel did not." I wanted to know how and why. The Ukrainian refinery attacks and the Gulf diesel exports arrive as a list.
- "face one": the antecedent (a ban) is several sentences back.
- "France, which holds the G7 presidency, called the leaders together; President Emmanuel Macron had said on September 18..." The tense and order are tangled.
- Trump was "thinking about" a ban on September 30, then said it "was never really ever on the table." The story never says these conflict.
- "within the first 20 days" and "the agency owes the G7 a report within 20 days" are two different 20-day clocks.
- "the only barrels anyone has offered beyond March are France's... 2 million" versus "France says it will put 10 million barrels of diesel into its own distributors' hands." The 2 and 10 are not reconciled.
- "Seven commodity ships crossed Hormuz... a hurricane shut in 62.9 percent": the hurricane comes from nowhere.
- The 40-row price table sits in the middle of the story.
- "In Europe diesel prices have doubled": no time frame and no source.

**4. Questions I still have**
- How much of the 100 million is actually diesel?
- Did the U.S. really threaten a ban, or not? Reuters sources say yes, and Macron says no.
- Will this move prices at all?
- What did Germany mean by "the market did not want them"?
- Who decided the March shares, and why are countries behind?
- Is the ban dead, or only paused?
- What is the 2 million vs 10 million France difference?

### Finding 1 — B1: Unsupported timing of price increases

**Page's exact words:**
> “fuel prices that have climbed since the war with Iran began.”

**Records and exact words:**

**E1/E3 — G7 Leaders' Statement, October 2, 2026:**
> “Facing unprecedented volatility in oil markets - with surging prices threatening economic stability and the well-being of our citizens”

The statement also says:
> “We condemn Iran’s continued attacks against its regional neighbours and its disruption of international trade, energy security and the global economy.” records

**E4 — EIA, U.S. No. 2 Diesel Retail Prices:**
- February 23, 2026: $3.809 per gallon.
- October 5, 2026: $6.199 per gallon. records records

**What a reader would wrongly believe:**

That the cited records establish when the war with Iran began and document fuel-price increases specifically since that event.

The records establish rising oil prices, higher U.S. diesel prices, and Iranian attacks disrupting energy security. However, none identifies the beginning of a war with Iran or establishes the claimed timing of price increases relative to that beginning.

The overall increase in fuel prices is supported; the specific war-start reference is not established by the records.

**1. Five sentences**

The Army wants to blow up 68 drums of degraded lead azide at its Iowa ammunition plant, saying the explosive is too unstable to move. That blast would send a lead-bearing plume across eastern Iowa and parts of Illinois. It was set for Friday, and the acting Army secretary pushed it to Monday after Illinois and Iowa officials, the governor and a lawsuit pressed him. Two environmental groups have asked a federal court to suspend the EPA's emergency permit, saying the Army skipped safer methods and the public got no comment period. The Army says it began warning people in March, but its own records show the first real warnings came in October and the plume maps only five days before the blast.

**2. What it's really about**

It's about whether the Army told the public about the danger in time, more than whether the blast itself is safe.

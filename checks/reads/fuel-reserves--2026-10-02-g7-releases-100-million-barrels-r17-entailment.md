### Finding 1 — B5: Diesel release order stated more definitively than the records support

**Page's exact words:**
> "with diesel going out first"

**Record E1 — G7 Leaders' Statement, October 2, 2026:**
> "including a frontloaded substantial diesel release within the first 20 days by G7 members and partners."

**Record E7 — International Energy Agency, October 7, 2026:**
> "IEA Members also supported the prioritisation of the release of diesel stocks, to the extent possible, given the current tightness in diesel markets."

records records

**What a reader would wrongly believe:**

That the G7 established a definite sequence requiring diesel to be released before crude oil or other products.

The G7 committed to a substantial, front-loaded diesel release within 20 days. The IEA subsequently supported prioritising diesel **to the extent possible**. Neither establishes that diesel must precede all other releases.

The page turns a commitment to prioritise and accelerate diesel releases into an unconditional statement about their order.

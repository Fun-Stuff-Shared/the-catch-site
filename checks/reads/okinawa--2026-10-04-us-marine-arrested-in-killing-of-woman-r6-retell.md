## 1. Five sentences

1. Japanese police arrested Lance Cpl. Devin Ballard, 20, in Okinawa on suspicion of strangling Anna Yagi, 39, in a Naha hotel room and taking her wallet and backpack. He denies it.
2. Police followed hotel camera images of a man leaving with her things. They found him the next night on a bar street in Okinawa City and arrested him before dawn.
3. Japan holds him because its police got to him first. Prosecutors have up to 23 days to decide whether to charge him.
4. Japan's foreign and defense ministers protested. The U.S. ordered a pause and a curfew, and its Okinawa commander expressed sympathy but did not apologize.
5. Okinawa's new governor says U.S. discipline and education have failed and wants the status of forces agreement reviewed, as Okinawa has asked after earlier crimes.

## 2. What it's really about

Whether Okinawa and Tokyo will finally force a change in the rules governing U.S. troops, after decades of crimes followed by patches that haven't stopped them.

## 3. Where I got lost

- **"so Japan holds him" / "on this suspected crime"** (dek). The cause and the clause read oddly before I know anything.
- **"Police said no handover question arose."** I didn't know who would have asked, or for what.
- **"Japanese police" and "Okinawa police."** I couldn't tell if these are the same force.
- **"Koja ... elected ... with the backing of Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's Liberal Democratic Party."** I didn't see why this matters until much later.
- **"the commander of U.S. forces in Japan."** He is never named, but another commander, Watson, is. Watson is "its commander on Okinawa," then "the top U.S. commander on Okinawa." I wasn't sure whether those are one person or two.
- **"Anna Yagi lived in the Kokuba neighborhood of Naha"** and **"Wakasa district."** Neither place name does anything later.
- **"Police say Yagi's wallet and backpack were found near the hotel on October 3."** This lands between his enlistment and the prosecutors, and it isn't tied to the camera footage.
- **The brother's statement, then "many posts on X ... blaming her."** The jump to online blame is abrupt. The governor's reply is the only link.
- **"How it came to this."** It opens with a Koja quote and then drops into "The United States governed Okinawa from the end of World War II." I lost the thread.
- **"Kenneth Franklin Gadson, a former Marine then working with the U.S. forces as a civilian ... a supplementary pact ... on which civilians the agreement covers."** I didn't see why the civilian question belongs here.
- **"101 criminal cases ... cleared."** "Cleared" is jargon.
- **"in 2003 it began a campaign across Japan."** Why 2003?
- **"a governor ... who accepts Tokyo's plan to keep the Futenma base on Okinawa but move it to a less populated area."** "Who" could refer to the party or the governor. The Futenma plan is never explained.
- **"Asked about revising the agreement in 2025, Motegi said..."** This jumps back a year in the middle of current events.
- **"The catch" section.** It is about press errors ("The Associated Press's first story, and NPR's copy of it"), and I wasn't told why I should care. The table's columns are out of order: claim, outlet, then the count.
- **"Japan holds him because its police arrested him off base"** against **"Because he was off duty and the victim was Japanese, Japan has the first right to try him."** Custody and the right to try are two different things, and the page doesn't separate them.
- **The sources list.** Mostly Japanese-language titles. Names like Jost, Tamaki and Oguchi appear only there, never in the story.

## 4. Questions I still had

- Did he know her? How did they meet, and what was the motive?
- What does "denies it" mean in his own words?
- How did police tie a man on camera at the hotel to a man in a bar the next night? Was the tattoo and shaved head the only link?
- Was he on liberty? Did he break any existing U.S. rule, such as the 1–5 a.m. bar ban?
- Which specific measures does Koja say failed, and what evidence shows they failed?
- What would revising the agreement change in this kind of case?
- What does the governor's backing from Takaichi's party have to do with what comes next?
- Will the assembly resolution pass on October 9? Will the Okinawa Policy Council meet?
- Who was Yagi, beyond her age and neighborhood?
- Why did the U.S. skip an apology when it gave one in 2012?

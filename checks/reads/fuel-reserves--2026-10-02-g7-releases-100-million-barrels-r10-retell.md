**1. Five sentences**

In March, 32 countries in the International Energy Agency (IEA) pledged to release 400 million barrels of emergency oil, and about 325 million has gone out. Iran's war has choked Hormuz, and U.S. diesel hit a record, so on October 2 the G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels over four months, diesel first. Trump had been threatening to ban U.S. diesel exports, and he said he wouldn't after the G7 deal. The White House calls it 100 million barrels of new European diesel. The IEA and Germany say it mostly finishes the unreleased March pledge, and Europe hasn't yet decided who releases what.

**2. What it's about**

It's about whether the G7's "100 million barrels" is new oil or the March pledge relabeled, and who gets to say so in a US–Europe fight over diesel.

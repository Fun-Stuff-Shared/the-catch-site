**1. Five sentences**
- The U.S. set up a Treasury account in January where buyers of Venezuelan oil pay, and Trump's order says the money belongs to Venezuela.
- A State Department report, made public November 12, says $12.26 billion came in through September 30 and $12.11 billion went out.
- Almost all of it went out as dollars sold through Venezuelan banks, at the request of Venezuela's central bank. Only about $133 million went straight to vendors, and none went to a U.S. agency.
- The report doesn't name who bought the dollars or at what rate. It says KPMG's work is not an audit, even though Rubio said KPMG audits every disbursement.
- Trump keeps saying the oil has paid for the war many times over and can go to the military. The report shows no payment to the U.S. from this account, and Democrats are still asking where the money went.

**2. What it's really about**
The administration has described this money in ways that don't match the record: it called the audit independent and says the oil pays for the war, but the report shows no audit and no payment to the U.S.

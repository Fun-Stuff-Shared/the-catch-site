**What it's really about:** DOJ is acting on a months-old, unreviewed-in-practice list while publicly calling it verified, and it is doing so days before the election.

- How did the county pick its 200? The story says it doesn't know.
- What does "set aside" mean in practice? The story says noncitizen ballots aren't counted and citizens' go through provisional steps, but not who decides or how fast.
- Why was the list delivered Oct. 13 when USCIS had the roll by May? Essayli's answer is given, then argued against. No one explains it.

- How do the 81,336 and 190,832 DHS numbers relate to the 20,000? The story says no filing connects them, so the reader gets two big numbers and no use for them.

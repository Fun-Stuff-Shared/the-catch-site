**1. Five sentences**
- Companies asked Commerce for licenses to sell about 3.4 million Nvidia and AMD AI chips to Chinese buyers. By May 11, Commerce had approved 879,868 of them, and the department's own table says none had shipped.
- The House Foreign Affairs Committee made that table public on November 19, a week of pressure on Commerce over Nvidia and China.
- Several outlets reported the approvals as chips already sent to China. Others reported the 3.4 million requested as if Commerce had approved it.
- Licensed H200s did reach China later, about $2.4 billion of Nvidia products in August to October, but no record says how many chips or under which licenses. Nvidia said on November 17 that it has stopped.
- Four of the approved buyers, Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu and BYD, are on the Pentagon's Chinese-military-company list, and together they got 272,000 chips. The Pentagon list doesn't bar exports.

**2. What it's really about**
It corrects the press: "approved" got reported as "shipped," and no public record shows how much actually went.

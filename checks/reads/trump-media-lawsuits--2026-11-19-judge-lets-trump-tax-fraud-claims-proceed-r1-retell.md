**1. Five sentences**

1. On Nov. 19 a federal judge in Tampa let Trump's defamation suit against the NYT, three of its reporters and Penguin Random House continue on 7 of 34 statements, and dismissed the other 27.
2. Six of the seven are tax-fraud claims from the book *Lucky Loser*, so they are against Penguin and the authors, not the Times.
3. The only claim left against the Times itself is one Peter Baker sentence about Mafia ties and money-laundering penalties.
4. The judge's reasoning is that anyone who accuses a person of a complex crime without a qualified expert's opinion knows they don't know, which plausibly shows reckless disregard.
5. Press-freedom groups say that misreads the actual-malice standard, several outlets misreported which claims and defendants survived, and nothing about truth is decided, with an answer due Dec. 17.

**2. What it's really about**

A judge's new test for malice, which says no expert opinion on a complex crime means the publisher knew it didn't know, kept the suit alive, and much of the press couldn't say who is being sued over what.

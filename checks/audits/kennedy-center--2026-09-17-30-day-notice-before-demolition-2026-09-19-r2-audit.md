## Reconstructed Story

**Established:** The board approved a two-year main-building closure on August 13. The first vote occurred amid acknowledged gaps in the comparative financial material. **Broader institutional context:** The September board materials connected closure and renovation deliberations with a proposal linking Trump recognition to fiscal rescue, fundraising, and renovation oversight. That connection is documented, but its causal role remains unproved.

1. **Major: The page turns permission to seek discovery into court-ordered discovery.**

   - Page bytes, offset 15633: “Beatty's counsel said Cooper also ordered discovery into the board's actions related to a potential demolition.”
   - Page bytes, offset 9931: “The federal docket's September 17 minute order was not available as a court-hosted document in the public sources we could preserve.”
   - Record bytes, [CourtListener docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/72069932/beatty-v-trump/): “Finally, Plaintiff may seek discovery regarding the subject of their 85 Notice of Supplemental Authority to the extent permitted by the Federal Rules and the Court's scheduling order.”
   - Correct account: Cooper permitted Beatty to seek discovery within the existing rules and schedule. He did not order defendants to produce discovery, and the page should not repeat counsel's broader characterization without correcting it from the primary docket text.

2. **Major: The page omits the filed Delta report and its non-sensitive appendices while relying on summaries of that report.**

   - Page bytes, offset 40682: “Floca's declaration identifies a JLL report and Delta presentation filed under seal; they were not available through the public storage route.”
   - Record bytes, [Floca declaration](/Volumes/4/GitHub/the-catch-site-wt-sol/data/sources/kennedy-center/floca-declaration-89-3-2026-09-18.txt:64), offsets 3130 and 3240: “correct copy of that report and certain of its appendices is attached to this declaration as” and “Exhibit A. Exhibit A includes appendices relied upon by the Delta Consulting Group to”.
   - Correct account: The earlier JLL plan and Delta presentation were under seal, but Floca said the finalized Delta report and non-sensitive appendices were attached as Exhibit A. The manifest does not pin that exhibit, leaving the page's closure, cost, and safety account one evidentiary layer short.

**C32 — [ca-settlement]**

**Claim:** “Vitol and SK settled California's gasoline price-manipulation case for $50 million in 2024, without admitting wrongdoing; the state's 2020 complaint names Addison as the supervisor of Vitol's California trader.”

**Records and exact words:**

- **RECORD ca-ag-vitol-settlement-2024-07-10:** “Attorney General Rob Bonta today announced a $50 million settlement with gas trading firms, resolving allegations that Vitol, Inc. (Vitol) and SK Energy Americas, Inc., along with its parent company SK Trading International (SK), secretly worked together to tamper with and manipulate spot market prices for California gasoline.” records(10)
- **RECORD reuters-vitol-vance-2026-11-11:** “Neither Vitol nor Addison admitted any wrongdoing.” records(10)
- **RECORD ca-complaint-vitol-2020:** “Lucas reported to John Addison (‘Addison’), a Vitol executive who in turn reported to the President of Vitol Americas.” records(10)

**Finding:** The records establish that Vitol and SK settled the case and that neither Vitol nor Addison admitted wrongdoing. They do not establish whether SK admitted wrongdoing. The claim extends the no-admission finding to both companies.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the records establish both Vitol and SK settled without admitting wrongdoing, when the cited confirmation covers Vitol and Addison, not SK.

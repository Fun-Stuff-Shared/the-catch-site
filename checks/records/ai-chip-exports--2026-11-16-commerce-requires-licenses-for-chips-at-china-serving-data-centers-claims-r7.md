### Finding: C2 — Scope exceeds the record

**Claim C2 [installed-chips]:**
> The data-center control reaches chips already installed abroad, not only new exports: it applies to whoever holds the chips, and a data center that starts serving such companies after the chips arrive needs a license for that change.

**Record: fr-pi-2026-23529 — Federal Register, November 16, 2026**

Section 744.24(a)(1), Note 1, states:

> The prohibitions pursuant to paragraph (a)(1) apply regardless of whether the person in possession of the EAR item was a party to the transaction to the original export, reexport, or transfer (in-country). 

However, the prohibition applies to specified controlled items when the relevant export, reexport or transfer involves a data center providing or expected to provide **more than 12,000,000 aggregate TPP** to covered companies. 

Section 744.24(e) further states:

> The notification from the exporter, reexporter, or transferor must also specify that the transferor, e.g., the data center operator, must obtain authorization for this change in end-use. 

Section 744.24(b)(2) also establishes an exception for qualifying commercial recommender systems when the required information is submitted. records(20261118-021753)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:**

The claim's statement that the control "applies to whoever holds the chips" could be understood to mean possession alone triggers licensing obligations. The record instead addresses prohibited exports, reexports and transfers, including qualifying changes in end-use, regardless of participation in the original shipment.

The unqualified statement that a data center needs a license whenever it starts serving covered companies also omits the computing threshold and applicable exception.

The underlying point that previously exported or installed chips can become subject to the control through a subsequent prohibited end-use is supported.

The short form, **"Covers chips already installed,"** does not independently overstate that point.

**C1 [below-threshold]:** No discrepancy found. The 12-million-TPP threshold, BIS notification authority and existing control involving a primary remote user exceeding 50% are supported by the record.

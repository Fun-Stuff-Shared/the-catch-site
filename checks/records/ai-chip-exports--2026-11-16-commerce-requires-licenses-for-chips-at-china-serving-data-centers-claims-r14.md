**C2. [comments-60-days]**

**Claim:** “BIS takes public comments until January 19, 2027, 60 days after publication.”

**Record fr-2026-23529:**
> “Comments on the revisions and additions in this rule must be received by BIS no later than January 19, 2027.” 

**Record fr-pi-2026-23529:**
> “BIS no later than [INSERT DATE 60 DAYS AFTER DATE OF PUBLICATION IN THE FEDERAL REGISTER].” 

**Finding:** The final rule confirms January 19, 2027, as the comment deadline. However, November 19, 2026, to January 19, 2027, is **61 calendar days**, not 60. The 60th day is January 18, 2027.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That January 19 falls exactly 60 days after publication. The deadline itself is correct, but the stated interval does not match the calendar dates.

C1 holds. The published Federal Register record confirms both November 19, 2026, as the effective date and the citation 91 FR 74970. records(20261119-194801)

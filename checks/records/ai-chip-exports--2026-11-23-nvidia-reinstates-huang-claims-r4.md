### C15 — [stock-fell-33]

**Claim:** “Nvidia's stock fell nearly 33% from Huang's November 8 admission of an affair until the reinstatement.”

**Short form:** “Stock down nearly 33% since Nov. 8”

**Record:** `cnbc-stock-2026-11-23`

**Exact words:**
> “Shares of Nvidia closed up 31% following Huang's reinstatement. The stock had shed nearly 33% from the time Huang admitted to an extramarital affair on Nov. 8.”



**Finding:** The full claim is supported, but the short form changes its timeframe. The nearly 33% decline preceded the reinstatement announcement and subsequent 31% rally. “Stock down nearly 33% since Nov. 8” can be read as describing the stock's position after the rally.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** Nvidia remained nearly 33% below its November 8 level as of November 23–24, despite its substantial recovery following the announcement.

### C35 — [doj-megaspeed-case]

**Claim:** “The Justice Department leads a criminal investigation of Megaspeed, which Commerce opened in 2024.”

**Short form:** “DOJ's Megaspeed case: open”

**Record:** `doj-commerce-megaspeed-2026-11-17`

**Exact words:**
> “After identifying suspicious activity through proactive monitoring of potential sensitive semiconductor diversion, the Department of Commerce initiated an investigation into Megaspeed in 2024 for potential export control violations.”

> “Following this enforcement action, the Department of Justice took the lead role in the criminal investigation of Megaspeed.”

records(20261124-125507)

**Finding:** The claim conflates the Commerce Department's original investigation with the subsequent Justice Department-led criminal investigation.

Commerce initiated its investigation in 2024. The joint statement describes a May 2025 records request, followed by an on-site inspection and interview in June 2025. It then states that DOJ took the lead role in the criminal investigation.

The record does not establish that Commerce opened a criminal investigation in 2024.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** The Justice Department's criminal investigation began in 2024 under Commerce's authority, rather than DOJ taking the lead in a criminal investigation following Commerce's earlier enforcement work.

The short form is supported independently of this chronology problem.

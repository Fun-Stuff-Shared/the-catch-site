### C49 — [california-suit]

**Claim:** “A week earlier, on Oct. 15, the department sued California asking a court to bar the state from counting ballots from about 20,000 voters it says are likely noncitizens.”

**Short form:** “Oct. 15: DOJ asks court to bar counting about 20,000 California ballots.”

**Record:** `latimes-california-2026-10-15`

**Exact words:**
> “The Trump administration on Thursday sued California over its handling of voter fraud claims, asking a federal court to immediately bar the state from counting ballots from about 20,000 voters statewide — many in Los Angeles County — whom the administration alleges are ‘likely noncitizens.’” records

**Finding:** The full claim is supported, but its short form changes the unit being counted. The record identifies approximately 20,000 **voters**, not 20,000 ballots.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That approximately 20,000 ballots had been cast or were certain to be cast and were subject to the requested prohibition. The record establishes the number of voters flagged by the federal government, not the number of ballots cast by those voters.

### C43 — [no-deadline]

**Claim:** “The five-year limit for this charge runs from the 2023 statements, so prosecutors had until 2028 to bring it.”

**Record:** `usc-18-3282` states:

> “Except as otherwise expressly provided by law, no person shall be prosecuted, tried, or punished for any offense, not capital, unless the indictment is found or the information is instituted within five years next after such offense shall have been committed.” records

`floris-complaint-2026-10-20` identifies the alleged offenses as occurring on “July 19, 2023 and September 28, 2023.” records

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That prosecutors had until 2028 simply to file criminal charges, including a complaint. The statute specifically requires an **indictment or information** within five years; filing a criminal complaint does not itself satisfy that requirement. The two alleged offenses also have different five-year deadlines: July 19, 2028, and September 28, 2028, respectively, absent applicable exceptions.

The claim's reference to bringing the case obscures this distinction, particularly because the prosecution has already begun through a criminal complaint.

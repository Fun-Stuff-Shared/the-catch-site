### C29 — [no-doj-statement]

**Claim:**
> The Justice Department has issued no public statement of its own on Huang; its position comes through Trump's post and an unnamed Justice Department official, who told NBC News that Blanche told Trump the department had no evidence of wrongdoing by Huang or Nvidia.

**Record: `nbc-doj-official-2026-11-23`**
> Blanche spoke with Trump over the weekend and told him that the Justice Department had no evidence of any wrongdoing by either Huang or Nvidia, a Justice Department official told NBC News.

**Record: `dailybeast-blanche-2026-11-24`**

Attorney General Todd Blanche publicly addressed questions about Huang on Fox News on November 23.

> “That assumes there’s an investigation that’s open,” Blanche shot back. “And that’s never been discussed publicly.”

He also said:

> “There’s certain companies that work with Nvidia,” Blanche said. “One’s under indictment, and one’s under investigation. That’s different than Nvidia itself.”

And:

> “a lot of thoughtful process by administration officials went into this decision, and the right decision was reached, I believe.”

**Record: `examiner-doj-2026-11-23`**
> “The AG did speak with the President over the weekend,” a Justice Department official told reporters Monday. “The DOJ has no evidence of any wrongdoing by either Jensen Huang or Nvidia.”

  

**Finding:** The claim's categorical statement that the Justice Department has issued no public statement of its own is too broad. Blanche himself publicly discussed the matter, and a Justice Department official also spoke to reporters.

Blanche did not provide a comprehensive public account of Huang's potential criminal liability, but his comments constitute an additional public statement from the department's leadership.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the Justice Department and its attorney general have made no public comments addressing Huang's situation, leaving Trump's post and NBC's anonymous source as the only expressions of the department's position.

### C51 — [ballroom]

**Claim:**
> Nvidia pledged $25 million to Trump's planned White House ballroom.

records(20261124-125135)

**Finding:** The record reports that Nvidia *would pledge* $25 million, not that it had already made the pledge. The claim converts a reported prospective commitment into an accomplished fact.

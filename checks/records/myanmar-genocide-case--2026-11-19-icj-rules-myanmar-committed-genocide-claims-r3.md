**Short form:** “It did not directly and publicly incite genocide.”

**Record:** `icj-myanmar-press-release-2026-11-19`

> “Finds that the Republic of the Union of Myanmar has not directly and publicly incited the commission of genocide against the Rohingya group by private persons in northern Rakhine State in violation of its obligation under the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide.” 

**Additional record:** `icj-myanmar-judgment-2026-11-19`, paragraph 490:

> “The responsibility of the State under Article III (c) for incitement of its own agents or organs to commit genocide is established and absorbed by the finding that the State itself has committed genocide.”

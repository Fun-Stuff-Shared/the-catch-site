## The event

- Early on Saturday, October 3, 2026, a 39-year-old woman was found dead in a hotel room in the Wakasa district of Naha, Okinawa's capital. A hotel employee called emergency services at about 4:20 a.m. saying a woman was unconscious. [okinawatimes-arrest-2026-10-04: "３日午前４時２０分ごろ、沖縄県那覇市若狭１丁目のホテルで、従業員から「女性の意識がない」と１１９番通報があり"]
- Police say she entered the hotel with a man at about 2:10 a.m.; staff checked the room at about 4:15 a.m. when it had not been vacated after checkout time and found her body. [jiji-arrest-2026-10-04: "女性は午前２時１０分ごろ、男と２人でホテルに入った。"] [jiji-arrest-2026-10-04: "チェックアウト時刻を過ぎても退室しなかったため、従業員が午前４時１５分ごろ室内を確認したところ遺体を見つけた。"]
- Okinawa Prefectural Police treated it as a robbery-murder the same day and set up a special investigation headquarters of 197 officers at Naha police station. [okinawatimes-arrest-2026-10-04: "島尻哲也刑事部長をトップとする１９７人体制の特別捜査本部を那覇署に設置した"]
- At about 11:30 p.m. on October 3, an investigator spotted a man matching the security-camera description walking alone in an entertainment district of Okinawa City, questioned him, took him voluntarily to Okinawa police station, and made an emergency arrest. [okinawatimes-arrest-detail-2026-10-04: "３日午後１１時半ごろ、現場近くの防犯カメラ映像に映っていた人物の身体的な特徴に酷似した男が、沖縄市の歓楽街で一人で歩いているのを捜査員が発見。"] [okinawatimes-arrest-detail-2026-10-04: "職務質問し、沖縄署に任意同行を求めて緊急逮捕した。"] The arrest came in the early hours of October 4. [jiji-arrest-2026-10-04: "沖縄署に任意同行を求め、４日未明に緊急逮捕した。"] The U.S. military's own order dates the arrest October 4. [watson-memo-2026-10-06: "On Oct. 4, a U.S. Marine was arrested by Japanese authorities in connection with the death of an Okinawan woman in Naha."]
- The man arrested is Lance Cpl. Devin Jacob Ballard, 20, of Marine Corps Air Station Futenma, held on suspicion of robbery-murder. He told police "I don't know." [jiji-arrest-2026-10-04: "海兵隊上等兵デビン・ジェイコブ・バラード容疑者（２０）を強盗殺人容疑で逮捕した。"] [okinawatimes-arrest-detail-2026-10-04: "調べに「私は知りません」と容疑を否認しているという。"]
- On October 5 police referred the case to the Naha District Public Prosecutors Office. [mainichi-referral-2026-10-05: "デビン・バラード容疑者（20）を那覇地検に送検した。"] [stripes-koja-protest-2026-10-05: "The case was forwarded to the Naha Public Prosecutors Office on Monday, he said."] He has not been indicted (see What is next).

## The people it happens to

- The woman killed: Anna Yagi (屋宜杏奈), 39, of Kokuba, Naha. [mainichi-arrest-2026-10-04: "那覇市国場の屋宜杏奈（やぎあんな）さん（39）を何らかの方法で殺害し"] Police describe her occupation as unknown. [okinawatimes-arrest-2026-10-04: "那覇市に住む職業不詳の女性（３９）を殺害し"]
- How she died: the autopsy found suffocation from strangulation. [jiji-arrest-2026-10-04: "司法解剖の結果、死因は首を絞められたことによる窒息と判明した。"] A police spokesman told Stars and Stripes she died after Ballard allegedly pressed on her neck. [stripes-koja-protest-2026-10-05: "Yagi died of asphyxiation after Ballard allegedly “pressed on her neck,” a police spokesman told Stars and Stripes at police headquarters Monday."] Investigative sources say there was a cord-like mark on her neck. [jiji-referral-2026-10-05: "ひも状のもので絞められた跡があり"] RBC reports investigators are examining whether the room's telephone cord was used; this is a line of inquiry, not a finding. [rbc-phone-cord-2026-10-06: "捜査関係者によると男が電話のコードで首を絞めた可能性を含めて捜査しています。"]
- Before the hotel: according to people involved in the case, the two were seen together in an Okinawa City entertainment district and appear to have taken a taxi to Naha. [ryukyushimpo-referral-2026-10-05: "被害者とともに沖縄市から那覇市にタクシーで移動したとみられる。"]
- What was taken: police suspect he took her wallet and backpack. Police say both were found near the hotel on October 3. [yomiuri-referral-2026-10-05: "県警によると、財布とリュックサックは３日にホテル周辺で見つかった。"] [okinawatimes-arrest-2026-10-04: "所持品はその後、ホテル周辺で見つかった。"]
- Her family: on the afternoon of October 4 her brother released a statement through police, addressed to the press: the family is grieving, their parents are exhausted by the loss of their daughter, and they asked to be left alone. [okinawatimes-family-statement-2026-10-04: "亡くなった女性（39）=同市=の兄が4日午後、県警を通じてコメントを公表した。"] [okinawatimes-family-statement-2026-10-04: "特に両親は娘を亡くし、憔悴しております。"] [okinawatimes-family-statement-2026-10-04: "どうか、私たち家族をそっとしておいてください。"]
- After her death: by October 6 many posts on X and other platforms blamed her own conduct for what happened; Koja said "there is no such thing as fault when a person has been killed" and asked people to stop, and civic groups called it secondary victimization. [mainichi-victim-blaming-2026-10-06: "女性の行動に落ち度があり、事件に巻き込まれたと責める内容の投稿が多数見られる。"] [mainichi-victim-blaming-2026-10-06: "人が殺されたということに対して、落ち度も何もあったものではない。慎んでいただきたい"] [mainichi-victim-blaming-2026-10-06: "米兵と接点があったとしても殺されていい理由にはならない"]
- The suspect: Ballard enlisted on August 8, 2024, trained at Parris Island, and was promoted to lance corporal on October 1, 2025, according to the service record III MEF gave Stars and Stripes. [stripes-curfew-2026-10-06: "He enlisted on Aug. 8, 2024, and completed recruit training at Parris Island, S.C., according to a copy of his service record provided Tuesday by III MEF spokesman 1st Lt. Tyler Thomas."] [stripes-curfew-2026-10-06: "He was promoted to lance corporal on Oct. 1, 2025."] He is a combat engineer with Marine Wing Support Squadron 172. [stripes-curfew-2026-10-06: "Ballard, a combat engineer assigned to Marine Wing Support Squadron 172, Marine Aircraft Group 36, 1st Marine Aircraft Wing, is being held by Japanese authorities."]
- How he was identified: a shaved head and distinctive tattoos on both arms. [mainichi-arrest-2026-10-04: "両腕にある特徴的なタトゥーの柄と、そり上げた髪形が本人特定につながった。"] When found he was wearing different clothes from those on the footage. [yomiuri-referral-2026-10-05: "３日深夜に捜査員がバラード容疑者を沖縄市内で発見した際には違う服装だったが"]
- Where he was found: Gate 2 Street in Okinawa City, the district where joint U.S.-Japanese patrols have run since April 2025, crowded with U.S. service members on a weekend night; plainclothes officers had been posted in entertainment districts in Naha and Okinawa City. [okinawatimes-gate-street-2026-10-04: "週末の３日午後１１時半ごろ、米兵らでにぎわう沖縄市のゲート通り。"] [stripes-solo-patrols-paused-2026-03-04: "Joint patrols were introduced in Okinawa city on April 19 to reduce crime and misconduct involving U.S. service members in Okinawa’s entertainment districts"]
- Custody: because Japanese police arrested him off base, the question of handing a suspect over under the status of forces agreement did not arise, police said. [okinawatimes-arrest-detail-2026-10-04: "県警による基地外での逮捕のため、日米地位協定を巡る身柄引き渡しの問題は生じていないという。"] His Marine Corps duty status is "confined foreign civilian authority." ## Why: the stated reasons

- Japan's government protested because of what Takaichi called an "extremely brutal and heinous" crime, and asked for maximum cooperation, discipline and prevention. [takaichi-x-2026-10-04: "このように極めて残忍で凶悪な事件の発生は極めて遺憾であり"] [takaichi-x-2026-10-04: "日本政府として米側に強く抗議するとともに、捜査への最大限の協力、米軍関係者の綱紀粛正と事件・事故の再発防止の徹底を強く求めたところです"]
- Koizumi explained why he acted the same day: a life was lost, and in the current security environment Japan must show a U.S.-Japan deterrent without a gap, so this must never happen again. [okinawatimes-koizumi-2026-10-04: "安全保障環境を鑑みれば、一点の隙もない日米の抑止力を対外的にも示すことは不可欠。"]
- Koja's stated reason for protest: residents living beside bases are shocked and newly afraid, and repeated recurrence shows U.S. discipline and education "are not functioning at all." [ap-leaders-2026-10-05: "If convicted, he could face life in prison or the death penalty."] Japanese law caps custody from arrest to the indictment decision at 23 days for a single offense (judicial detention of up to 10 days, extendable by 10). [moj-criminal-justice-faq: "The maximum period of holding suspects in custody after their arrest until the decision on whether or not to indict them is 23 days in any single crime"] [moj-criminal-justice-faq: "The period of detention may be extended by up to 10 days only if the judge finds that there are unavoidable circumstances."] Counting from the October 4 arrest, the decision on this charge falls by late October unless prosecutors re-arrest on a separate charge; the detention order itself is not in the record.

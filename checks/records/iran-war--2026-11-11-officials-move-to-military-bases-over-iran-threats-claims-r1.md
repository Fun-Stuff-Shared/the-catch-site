**Claim:** “Israel Hayom reported on November 14, citing unnamed Israeli and U.S. officials, that Rubio ‘had also been moved to secure housing on a military base’ in response to Iranian threats.”

**Contradictory chronology in the record:**

**Problem:** Israel Hayom attributed intelligence about Iranian threats to unnamed Israeli and American officials. Its information about Rubio's military housing was presented as part of another report, immediately following a reference to Fox News.

Israel Hayom's general assertion that several officials relocated in response to Iranian threats does not establish that Rubio's original relocation was prompted by those threats. Nor does it establish a new November 2026 move.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That Israeli and American officials independently confirmed Rubio had recently moved to military housing because of Iranian threats associated with the ongoing war, rather than that his residence on a military base was documented before the war began.

**Four claims have support or scope problems.** The remaining claims hold against their cited records.

**C10 — [nationwide-comms-failure]**

**Claim:** “At 4:21 p.m. the FAA's command center put every en-route center on longer flight-plan times because of what it called a nationwide communications failure.”

**Record:** `atcscc-advzy-117-2026-11-13`

**Exact words:** “DCC HAS IMPLEMENTED EXTENDED FLIGHT PLAN DROP TIMES TO 240 MINUTES FOR ALL ENROUTE CENTERS DUE TO NATIONWIDE COMMS FAILURE.” records(20261114-023659)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the FAA extended flight-plan durations or flight times. The advisory specifically extended *flight-plan drop times*—the period before flight plans are dropped from the system—to 240 minutes. The claim loses that operational distinction. The short form accurately preserves the command center's description of a nationwide communications failure.

**C43 — [fti-multi-carrier]**

**Claim:** “L3Harris says FTI leases from several local carriers for redundancy and is upgraded in maintenance windows with parallel systems.”

**Record:** `l3harris-fti-page-2026-11-13`

**Exact words:** “The FTI network leverages multiple local exchange carriers for robust and dependable connections. This multi-carrier approach ensures redundancy and reliability across the system.”

The company also states: “We maintain 99.99971% network reliability by working during maintenance windows, deploying parallel systems and coordinating closely with FAA technical operations staff.” records(20261114-023659)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That L3Harris specifically disclosed leasing telecommunications capacity from those carriers. The record establishes use of multiple local exchange carriers but does not specify the contractual arrangement. The remainder of the claim is supported.

**C49 — [flightaware-715]**

**Claim:** “By 7:15 p.m. Eastern, FlightAware counted more than 4,000 cancellations and 10,000 domestic delays for the day.”

**Short form:** “4,000+ cancelled by 7:15 p.m.”

**Record:** `axios-ground-stop-2026-11-13`

**Exact words:** “As of 7:15 p.m. ET, FlightAware reported more than 10,000 domestic delays and 4,000 cancellations over the course of the day Friday.” records(20261114-023659)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That Axios explicitly established cancellations had exceeded 4,000 by 7:15 p.m. Its wording states 4,000 cancellations; the explicit “more than” qualifier applies to the 10,000 domestic delays. The record therefore does not establish the greater-than-4,000 claim. The short form is compatible with a count of at least 4,000.

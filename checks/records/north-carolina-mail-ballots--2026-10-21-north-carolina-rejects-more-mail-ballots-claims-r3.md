### C3 — [dd-no-note]

**Claim:** “The first version and the current one end with the same line, ‘This story has been updated.’, and neither says what changed.”

**Records:**
- `democracy-docket-2026-10-20-archived`
- `democracy-docket-2026-10-20`

**Exact words in both records:**
> This story has been updated.  
> *The Elias Law Group (ELG) represents the plaintiffs in this case. ELG Firm Chair Marc Elias is the founder of Democracy Docket.

**Finding:** Two problems.

First, neither record ends with the quoted update notice. Both contain an additional disclosure identifying the plaintiffs' law firm and its connection to Democracy Docket.

Second, the archived record is identified as a snapshot from **October 21, 2026, at 18:38 UTC**. Nothing establishes that it represents the article's first published version. It already incorporates Stein's Tuesday evening statement, so at least some updating may have occurred before that snapshot.

The narrower observation that neither version explains what changed is supported.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the archived copy is the original published article and that the update notice is literally the final line of both versions. The records establish neither proposition.

The other four claims hold against their cited records.

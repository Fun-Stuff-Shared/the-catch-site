### C37 — [rodriguez-99] — Percentage conflicts with accompanying figures

**Claim:** “Rodríguez said 99 of every 100 dollars of oil and mining income from January to November went to public-sector wages and pensions: by her government's figures, $14.710 billion of $15.603 billion taken in, on a portal that covers two government funds.”

**Records:** `contrapunto-rodriguez-2026-11-12` and `eldiario-rodriguez-2026-11-12`

**Exact record words:**

Contrapunto:
> «De cada 100 dólares ingresados por concepto de petróleo y minería entre enero y noviembre de 2026, 99 se destinaron al pago de salarios de los trabajadores del sector público y de pensiones»

> En la página se detalla que, hasta el 12 de noviembre, ha habido un total de $ 15.603 millones en ingresos y $ 14.860 en egresos ($ 14.710 millones para pagos de trabajadaores y pensiones y $ 150,5 millones fue a gastos de funcionamiento de gobierno).

El Diario:
> Este portal cubre únicamente el Fondo de Protección Social y el Fondo de Infraestructura Nacional de Venezuela. No incluye otros fondos o programas gubernamentales.

records(20261113-195923) records(20261113-195923)

**Finding:** The claim accurately attributes the 99% statement to Rodríguez and reproduces the reported amounts. However, the figures do not substantiate that percentage of income.

The reported $14.710 billion represents **94.3% of $15.603 billion in income**, not 99%. It represents approximately 99% of the reported $14.860 billion in expenditures.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the government's published income and spending figures corroborate Rodríguez's statement that 99% of oil and mining income went to wages and pensions. The figures instead support approximately 99% of expenditures, or 94.3% of income.

The attribution itself holds; the discrepancy is in the implied numerical relationship.

**Claim:** “The rule's data-center control covers exports, reexports and in-country transfers of chips to data centers outside the United States.”

**Short form:** “Covers chips sent to data centers abroad.”

**Record:** `fr-pi-2026-23529`

**Exact words (§ 744.24, lines 339–349):**
> “You may not knowingly export, reexport, or transfer (in-county) items specified in paragraph (a) of § 744.24 for use in a data center to or within any destination worldwide that will provide access to more than 12,000,000 ‘aggregate TPP’ [...] to companies headquartered in, or whose ultimate parent company is headquartered in, Macau or a destination specified in Country Group D:5”



**Additional record context:** The claim's cited passage concerning “data centers outside of the United States” appears in the explanation of § 744.23(a)(3), a separate end-use control. The new § 744.24 explicitly uses “any destination worldwide.” records(20261118-033042)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the new data-center control is geographically limited to facilities outside the United States. The operative provision specifies worldwide destination coverage, subject to defined items, knowledge, computing-power thresholds, and qualifying end users. Neither the claim nor its short form preserves that scope.

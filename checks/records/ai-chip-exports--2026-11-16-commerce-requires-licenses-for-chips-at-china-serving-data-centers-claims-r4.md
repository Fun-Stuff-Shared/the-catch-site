### C5 — Short form broadens the licensing exemption

**Claim:** “The rule requires a license to export advanced AI chips to or within Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Brazil and Thailand, except when the ultimate consignee or end user is headquartered in the United States, or has a U.S.-headquartered ultimate parent, and the exporter files reports to BIS.”

**Short form:** “License for shipments to five countries, unless the buyer is U.S.-based and reported.”

**Record:** `fr-pi-2026-23529`, § 742.6(a)(6)(iii)(A)(1), Note 1:

> “The license requirements in this paragraph (a)(6)(iii)(A)(1) do not apply to exports, reexports, and transfers (in-country) to an ultimate consignee or end user headquartered in, or whose ultimate parent company is headquartered in, the United States, provided the exporter, reexporter, or transferor follows the reporting requirements in § 743.10.” 

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That having a U.S.-based buyer is sufficient to qualify for the exemption. The regulation specifies the ultimate consignee or end user, or its ultimate parent, rather than any buyer. A U.S.-based purchaser does not necessarily qualify a shipment for the exemption. The short form also obscures that reporting is the responsibility of the exporter, reexporter or transferor.

The full claim is supported. Its short form is not.

### C29 — Unenforced restrictions presented as operative

**Claim:** “Biden's January 2025 rule restricted remote access to data centers; in May 2025 Trump's Commerce Department said it would not enforce it and would rescind it.”

**Record:** `nyt-loophole-2026-11-16`:

> “Just before leaving office, the Biden administration issued global rules on chip sales, including restrictions on Chinese firms remotely accessing data centers.”

**Record:** `bis-diffusion-rescission-2025-05-13`:

> “The AI Diffusion Rule was issued on January 15, 2025, with compliance requirements that were set to come into effect on May 15, 2025.”

The same BIS release states that enforcement officials had been instructed not to enforce the rule. 

**Record:** `fr-pi-2026-23529`:

> “The AI Diffusion Rule has never been enforced.” records(20261118-020606)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That Biden's January 2025 restrictions operated for several months before the Trump administration stopped enforcing them. The record instead establishes that the relevant compliance requirements were scheduled for May 15, 2025, and the rule was never enforced.

The claim accurately identifies the earlier rule and the subsequent non-enforcement decision, but its description of the restrictions as already operative creates a misleading understanding of the sequence.

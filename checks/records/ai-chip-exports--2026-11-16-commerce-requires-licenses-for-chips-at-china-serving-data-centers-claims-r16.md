### C2. [direct-ban-2022] — Incorrect effective date

**Claim:** “Since October 7, 2022, BIS has required a license to export, reexport or transfer the advanced computing chips it identified to or within China.”

**Record:** `fr-2022-21658`

The Federal Register distinguishes two effective dates:

- **October 7, 2022:** “A license is required for items specified in ECCN 3B090 and associated software and technology” — covering specified semiconductor manufacturing equipment.
- **October 21, 2022:** “Effective on October 21, 2022, § 742.6 is further amended by revising paragraphs (a)(6) and (b)(10).” The amended provision states: “A license is required for items specified in ECCNs 3A090, 3B090, 4A090” and other identified items.

The record also explicitly schedules the addition of ECCN 3A090, covering advanced computing integrated circuits, for October 21, 2022.  

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That BIS's October 7, 2022, licensing requirements already covered the newly identified advanced computing chips. The record establishes October 21, 2022, as the effective date for those chip-specific licensing requirements.

The short form, “Oct 2022: licenses for advanced chips to China,” does not independently misstate the effective month.

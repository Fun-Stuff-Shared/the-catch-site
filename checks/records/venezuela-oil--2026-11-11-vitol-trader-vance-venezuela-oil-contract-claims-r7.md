**C10 — [iraq-2003-tender]**

**Claim:** “In 2003, under U.S. administration, occupied Iraq's oil exports were sold only by its state marketer SOMO, with proceeds into an audited fund; its first post-war sale, of 10 million stored barrels, went by tender to six refiners out of 52 bidders.”

**Finding:** Two scope problems:

**Wrong reader takeaway:** That all Iraqi oil exports passed through the official state marketer and that the June 2003 tender was definitively the first oil sale after the invasion. The records establish neither proposition without qualification.

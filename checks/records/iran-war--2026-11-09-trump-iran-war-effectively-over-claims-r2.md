**C6 — [centcom-iron-gate] — Scope exceeds the record**

**Claim:** “On November 7, Central Command set up Task Force Iron Gate, a staff of about 20 in Tampa, to work with other agencies against Iranian shipments across its land borders and the Caspian Sea, and said enforcement at sea continues against all ships entering or leaving Iranian ports.”

**Short form:** “Central Command sets up Task Force Iron Gate against Iran's land routes”

**Record:** `centcom-iron-gate-2026-11-07`

**Exact words:**
> “On Nov. 7, the commander of U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) directed the establishment of a task force that will support ongoing U.S. government efforts to prevent Iran from using ground routes to transport advanced weapons and other materials.”

> “The new CENTCOM team will coordinate with other U.S. government agencies on preventing the movement of advanced weapons systems, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials, and illegal revenue streams across Iran’s land borders and the Caspian Sea.” records(20261115-172312)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That Task Force Iron Gate targets Iranian shipments generally, potentially including lawful commercial shipments, rather than the specified weapons, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials and illicit revenue streams. The short form broadens this further by describing the task force as operating against Iran's land routes themselves.

The record also describes a directive to establish the task force and prospective interagency coordination, rather than confirming that its operations were already underway.

**C16 — [bahrain-nov15] — Short form attributes interception to an unconfirmed operator**

**Claim:** “On November 15, Bahrain's foreign ministry said Iranian drones were intercepted and destroyed by air defense systems in Bahrain's airspace, praised the Bahrain Defense Force for confronting the attack, and said Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed it and a similar attack on November 13.”

**Short form:** “Bahrain says its air defenses intercepted Iranian drones”

**Record:** `bahrain-mfa-2026-11-15`

**Exact words:**
> “including the attack launched today, Sunday, November 15, 2026, by drones that were intercepted and destroyed by air defense systems in the Kingdom’s airspace.”

> “The Ministry praised the efficiency, vigilance, and capability of the Bahrain Defense Force in confronting today’s attack and its complete readiness to deter any aggression.” records(20261115-172312)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That Bahrain's own air defense forces or systems were specifically responsible for intercepting the November 15 drones.

The ministry identifies where the interception occurred and praises the Bahrain Defense Force but does not explicitly identify who operated the intercepting systems.

That distinction matters because `centcom-drones-bahrain-2026-11-13` expressly attributes the November 13 interceptions to U.S. forces. Bahrain's November 15 statement does not make an equivalent attribution for that day's attack.

The full claim is supported; the short form exceeds what the record establishes.

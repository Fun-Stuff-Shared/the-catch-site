### C21 — [rule-2024]

**Claim:** “Under the 2024 memo, a ballot counted without further action if either envelope arrived sealed, and was spoiled only if both arrived unsealed.”

**Record:** `schroeder-opinion-2026-10-19`

> “Under the 2024 Numbered Memo, a ballot was ‘spoiled’ – meaning it would not be counted and cannot be cured, requiring the voter to cast a new ballot – if both the outer envelope and the container-return envelope arrived unsealed, or appear to have been opened and re-sealed.”

> “However, if either the outer envelope or container-return envelope arrived sealed, then there was no deficiency, meaning the ballot would be counted without further action from the voter.”

**Finding:** The claim omits the alternative involving envelopes that appeared to have been opened and resealed. The statement that ballots were spoiled **only if both envelopes arrived unsealed** is narrower than the actual 2024 rule.

The short form, “2024: either envelope sealed was enough,” is supported.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the 2024 guidance required spoiling only when both envelopes were physically unsealed, excluding circumstances involving envelopes that appeared opened and resealed. records

### C47 — [reissue-id-again]

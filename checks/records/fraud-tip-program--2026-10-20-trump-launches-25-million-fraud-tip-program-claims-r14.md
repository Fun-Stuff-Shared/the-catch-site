### C1 — Two discrepancies

**Finding 1: The serious-harm exception is broader than the terms allow.**

**Claim words:**
> “The exceptions are a reasonable belief that reporting is needed to stop conduct likely to cause serious harm, such as violence or imminent harm to patients or others”

**Record:** `doj-whip-terms-2026-10-20`

**Exact words:**
> “has a reasonable basis to believe that disclosure of the information to the Department is necessary to prevent the relevant individual or entity from engaging in criminal conduct that is likely to harm national security, result in crimes of violence, result in imminent harm to patients in connection with health care, or cause imminent financial or physical harm to others”

records(20261025-035838)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That preventing any conduct likely to cause serious harm qualifies for the exception. The terms require criminal conduct likely to produce specified consequences. They also limit the patient-harm provision to health care and specify imminent financial or physical harm to others.

**Finding 2: The 120-day exception excludes categories of people the terms include.**

**Claim words:**
> “for officers, directors and compliance or audit staff only”

**Record:** `doj-whip-terms-2026-10-20`

**Exact words:**
> “is someone described in (iv)(a) or (iv)(b) and at least 120 days have elapsed”

Paragraph (iv)(a) includes:
> “an officer, director, trustee, or partner of an entity”

Paragraph (iv)(b) also includes people:
> “employed by or otherwise associated with a firm retained to perform compliance or internal audit functions for an entity”

records(20261025-035838)

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the 120-day exception is limited to officers, directors and compliance or audit staff. The terms also expressly include trustees, partners and qualifying personnel associated with outside compliance or internal-audit firms. The word “only” makes the exclusion misleading.

The remaining portions of C1 are supported at the level of precision required for a reader's takeaway.

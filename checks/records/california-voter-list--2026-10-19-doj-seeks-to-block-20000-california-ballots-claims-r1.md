### C41 — California's response to federal grand jury subpoenas

**Claim:** “California says it has answered federal grand jury subpoenas for the voter records of about 300 people since August.”

**Record:** `ca-state-opp-2026-10-20`

**Exact words:**
> “Since August 2026, the Secretary’s Office has received federal grand jury subpoenas for voter records for approximately 300 individuals and has provided, or is in the process of providing, responsive records to each of those subpoenas.” records

**Finding:** The claim presents California's responses as completed. The filing distinguishes between records already provided and records still being prepared or supplied.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That California had finished responding to the subpoenas covering approximately 300 individuals, when the state said some responses remained in progress.

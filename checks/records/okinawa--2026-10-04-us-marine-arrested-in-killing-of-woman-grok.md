## Stated reasons

* **Japanese officials’ stated reason for the protests** is the alleged robbery-murder itself and the fear it creates for people who live next to the bases. Takaichi, Motegi, Koizumi, the Defense Ministry, and Koja each said that in the words quoted above. They asked for cooperation, discipline, and prevention. Koja and the Naha council went on to demand a SOFA review. Motegi on October 6 did not commit to revision. He asked for talks on measures that work.
* **Glass’s stated reason for saying the SOFA is working,** October 6, is that the service member is in prefectural police custody and in the Japanese court system.
* **Watson’s stated reason for the pause and curfew,** October 6 memo: incidents like this require a pause. Conduct builds or damages trust, and damaged trust affects the alliance. Liberty is a privilege and a collective responsibility. The memo does not say alcohol was a factor in this case, and police accounts I read do not establish that it was.
* **The liberty rules already in force** date to a USFJ order effective October 1, 2024, signed by then-commander Lt. Gen. Ricky Rupp. The published order banned off-base public drinking and presence in off-base drinking establishments from 1 a.m. to 5 a.m. Stars and Stripes reported at the time that the review followed sexual-assault and attempted sexual-assault cases on Okinawa. On October 5, 2026, Severns told Stars and Stripes there was nothing new to announce and pointed back to that 2024 rule. The October 6 Watson order is narrower in geography, limited to Okinawa, and stricter: no off-base liberty for 48 hours, a total alcohol ban for those 48 hours, then a midnight-to-5 a.m. curfew for 30 days. https://www.stripes.com/theaters/asia_pacific/2024-09-30/japan-liberty-rules-off-base-drinking-15352162.html https://home.army.mil/japan/6917/5385/4056/1._Liberty_Policy_-_eff_1_Oct_24.pdf
* **Earlier killings that Koja’s October 5 letter cited,** as identified by Stars and Stripes: the 2016 killing of Rina Shimabukuro, for which former Marine Kenneth Franklin Gadson was later convicted, and the 2019 killing of a Japanese woman by Navy Petty Officer 3rd Class Gabriel Olivero, who then killed himself, according to police. After the 2016 arrest, the U.S. military imposed a month-long midnight curfew and an off-base alcohol ban on Okinawa. Those are prior cases the governor invoked. They are not evidence about what happened in the hotel on October 3.

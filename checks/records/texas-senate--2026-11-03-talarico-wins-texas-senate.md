## The story

James Talarico, a Democrat, won the Texas Senate seat on November 3, beating Republican Ken Paxton by about 12 points in unofficial returns. Republicans lost that seat, which they had held since 1961, along with all 17 other statewide races on the ballot. Vice President Vance and Sen. Ted Cruz blame the Iran war and prices for the party's losses, and Paxton, backed by Trump over Sen. John Cornyn, ran behind every other Republican. Republicans now argue whether Cornyn could have held the seat, which no poll settles, and outlets disagree about how much Elon Musk spent trying to stop Talarico. Counties finish counting by November 17, final money reports come December 3, and Talarico takes office in January.

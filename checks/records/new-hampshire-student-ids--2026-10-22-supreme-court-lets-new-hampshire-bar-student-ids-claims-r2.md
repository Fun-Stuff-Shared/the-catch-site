### Finding — C23 [registering-proof]

**Claim:** “Someone registering at the polls on election day must prove identity, age, citizenship and domicile, and from October 22 the state says a student ID cannot be used to prove identity.”

**Record:** `rsa-654-12`

The record establishes a specific exception to the four-proof requirement.

RSA 654:12, III states:

> “If the election official receiving the application confirms through the centralized voter registration database required by RSA 654:45 or other official records that the applicant was previously or is currently registered to vote in New Hampshire, the applicant shall prove identity and domicile, but shall not be required to prove his or her age or citizenship.” records

The exception applies to applicants previously or currently registered in a different New Hampshire town or ward. The statute's registration provisions apply both before and on election day.

The October 22 restriction on student IDs is supported by `sos-register-vote-2026-10-24`, which states:

> “Note: effective October 22, 2026, registrants cannot use student identification cards to prove identity.” records

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** Every person registering at the polls must independently prove all four qualifications. In fact, someone previously or currently registered in another New Hampshire town or ward need not reestablish age or citizenship if election officials confirm that registration. The claim presents the general requirement as universal, omitting an express statutory exception.

**Finding:** The 98.7% includes one natural-gas deposit, not exclusively oil and petroleum products. The total deposits and depositor count are supported.

**C50 — [no-record-billions-to-us]**

**Claim:** “None of the records this story holds shows billions of dollars from Venezuela's oil reaching the United States, in the account or outside it.”

**Record:** `state-custody-report-2026-11-12`, Sections 2.3 and 7.3.1.

The report states:

> “During the period covered by this report (January 3 – September 30, 2026), the Venezuela Custody Account received deposits totaling more than $12.26 billion from verified and approved entities engaging in authorized activities related to Venezuelan natural resources.” records(20261113-195504)

It also states:

> “Every dollar disbursed through the electronic FX intervention program therefore passes through the U.S. financial system, making an active U.S. correspondent banking relationship a prerequisite for participation in the program.” records(20261113-195504)

Executive Order 14373 separately establishes:

> “The Foreign Government Deposit Funds constitute property of the Government of Venezuela”

And specifies that the United States holds them:

> “solely in a custodial and governmental capacity” records(20261113-195504)

**Finding:** The claim conflates money reaching the United States with money becoming U.S. government property or revenue.

The records explicitly document billions of dollars entering U.S. Treasury custody and passing through American financial institutions.

They do not establish that those billions became U.S. government revenue available to finance military operations.

The claim's categorical statement that no records show billions reaching the United States is therefore contradicted by the custody and payment records themselves.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That the records show no substantial Venezuelan oil revenues entering U.S.-controlled accounts or the American financial system, when they actually document those flows while distinguishing Venezuelan ownership from American custody.

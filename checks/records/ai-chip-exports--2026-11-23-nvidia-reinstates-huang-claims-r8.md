**C1 — [trump-post-nov-22] — Unsupported timing of the board's vote**

**Claim:** “On November 22, the day of the board's vote, Trump posted that he hoped the board would rehire Huang.”

**Record:** `trump-nvidia-post-2026-11-22`

**Exact words:**
> “I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang. It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.”

The November 23 record states:
> “The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO.” records(20261124-130820)

**Finding:** The November 22 record supports the date and substance of Trump's post but does not establish that the board voted on November 22. The November 23 record confirms Trump acknowledged the board's decision by that date, without specifying when the vote occurred. Neither record establishes the vote's date.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That November 22 was the confirmed date of Nvidia's board vote, rather than merely the date of Trump's appeal for Huang's reinstatement.

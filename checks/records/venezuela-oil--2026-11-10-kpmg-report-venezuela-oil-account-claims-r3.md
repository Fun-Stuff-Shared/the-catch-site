### C15 — [recipients-split]

**Claim:** “By the banks' data, businesses received $9.37 billion of the dollars and individuals $2.87 billion; those totals also count $346 million Venezuela drew from the IMF, so they do not trace the account's money alone.”

**Record:** `state-custody-report-2026-11-12`

The report states:

> “The BCV receives funding from multiple sources, including $346 million that Venezuela withdrew from its reserve tranche at the IMF.”

It further states:

> “Recipient reporting reflects intervention activity executed through the participating commercial banks from the BCV operational account (inclusive of both Venezuela Custody Account and IMF funds).”

But it also specifies that the supplementary funds are:

> “not currently distinguishable within the recipient-level disbursement data provided by the BCV.” records(20261113-200424)

**Finding:** The record establishes that $346 million was withdrawn from the IMF and commingled with custody-account funds. It does not establish that the entire $346 million was distributed to downstream recipients by September 30 or included in the reported $9.37 billion and $2.87 billion totals.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That exactly $346 million in IMF funds is included in those recipient totals, when the report establishes only that the recipient data combines funding sources that cannot be distinguished.

### C31 — [account-nothing-to-us]

**Claim:** “The report lists no payment from the account to any U.S. government agency; its payouts went to Venezuelan banks and to vendors, at the Venezuelan central bank's direction.”

**Short form:** “Nothing paid to a U.S. agency.”

**Record:** `state-custody-report-2026-11-12`

The report states:

> “As of September 30, 2026, an undisbursed balance of $15.0 million ($15,000,526.20) remained in a BCV operational account.” records(20261113-200424)

It also states:

> “Out of $549,760,000.00 delivered for Banknote FX Interventions, $356,904,800.00 has been cleared and distributed. The balance of $192,855,200.00 remains pending authorization.” records(20261113-200424)

**Finding:** The claim's first clause and short form hold: the report lists no payment to a U.S. government agency. However, the description of the payouts as going to Venezuelan banks and vendors is broader than the completed distributions. Some money remained in a BCV operational account, and $192.9 million in physical currency had not yet been authorized for distribution.

**What a reader would wrongly believe:** That all money released from U.S. custody had reached Venezuelan commercial banks or vendors by September 30, when the report identifies substantial amounts awaiting further distribution.

# Product

## Platform
web

## Purpose
The Catch helps readers understand public events and inspect the records behind the reporting. The writing authority is WRITING.md. The July 2026 jobs story is the reference for editorial hierarchy and reading modes.

## Confirmed direction
Stories must be directly accessible from discovery. Events are ongoing series containing dated stories; series pages must accommodate later developments without fabricating any. Outlet articles are internal references, excluded from discovery in this design pass. Existing citations remain available in stories.

## Reader journey
Latest stories or a series → story → source passage in context → return to the same reading position. Readers can also open the complete saved source.

## Boundaries
This work changes UI, navigation, and discovery presentation. It preserves authored story prose, publication selection, source bytes, and evidence relationships. Future Fed reading modes and upstream state changes are outside this pass. Deployment requires separate authorization.

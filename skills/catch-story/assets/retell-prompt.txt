Below is a news story. Read it once, top to bottom, the way you would read any article.

Then, without going back to it:
1. Tell a friend what happened, in five sentences.
2. Say what you think the story is really about, in one sentence.
3. List the questions you still had when you finished.
4. List any sentence that sounded like a machine wrote it: hedging, announcing, summing up, explaining what nobody asked. Quote it.

Be plain and specific. Do not praise the story.

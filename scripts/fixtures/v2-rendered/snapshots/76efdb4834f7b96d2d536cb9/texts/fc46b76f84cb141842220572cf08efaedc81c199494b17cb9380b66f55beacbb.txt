 Methodology
 Sample Design
 This survey was conducted among a sample of 500 likely voters in Nebraska, fielded September 14–16, 2026. Respondents
 were recruited and surveyed online via an online panel. Respondents were screened for registration status and likelihood of
 voting in the 2026 general election.

 Weighting
 Data were weighted using iterative proportional fitting (raking) to known population targets for gender, age, race/ethnicity, and
 education, based on the Nebraska Secretary of State voter file and U.S. Census Bureau estimates of the state's registered
 voter population.

 Sponsorship & Independence
 This survey was conceived and designed by Wedgewood Polls and funded through a crowdfunding campaign organized by a
 group of individual contributors on Election Twitter. No candidate, campaign, party, or outside organization sponsored this
 release, and no funder had input into question wording, sample design, or the analysis presented here.

 Margin of Error
 At the 95% confidence level, the statewide sample of 500 likely voters carries a margin of error of ±4.4 percentage points.
 Margins of error for individual crosstabulation subgroups are larger and increase as subgroup size decreases.

 Quality Controls
 Standard data quality measures were applied throughout fielding, including speeder and straight-lining detection, open-end
 response review, and removal of duplicate or inconsistent respondents prior to weighting and analysis.

 For questions regarding methodology, full topline results, or media inquiries, contact Wedgewood Polls.

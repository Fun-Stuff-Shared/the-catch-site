On October 15, 2026, the United States filed its complaint in this action, seeking
statewide declaratory and injunctive relief to enjoin the state of California from
continuing to send and tabulate vote-by-mail ballots cast by ineligible noncitizens to
illegally participate in federal elections. Dkt. 1.
On October 19, 2026, the United States filed an emergency ex parte application
for injunctive relief, including a temporary restraining order and preliminary injunction. 1
Dkt. 62. The United States concurrently requested an expedited ruling on its emergency
application by 12:00 p.m. on October 22, 2026. Dkt. 67. The Court has not ruled on the
United States’ application.
Given the critical and exigent nature of the relief sought, the Court’s silence is a
constructive denial of the United States’ emergency application for preliminary
injunctive relief in its Ex Parte Application for Temporary Restraining Order and Order
to Show Cause Why a Preliminary Injunction Should Not Issue, Dkts. 22-1, 62, which
has the practical effect of denying a preliminary injunction. Accordingly, the United
States hereby appeals the constructive denial of an injunction to the Ninth Circuit Court
of Appeals.

# Social brand reference

These are the assets The Catch already uses on its social accounts. They're here for reference only; nothing outside `public/` is served by the site.

- `social/`: avatar and banners. Black ground, a serif wordmark, cream "paper" documents, a yellow highlight on the line that matters, and a strikethrough on the claim that was wrong.
- `cards/`: X quote cards and reel frames. Cream paper texture, red all-caps source labels, bold serif quotes, a yellow highlight on the key words, and the site domain at the foot.

The site should feel like the same publication as these, without copying them literally.
